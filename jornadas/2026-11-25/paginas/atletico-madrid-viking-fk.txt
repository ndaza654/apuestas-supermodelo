FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Atlético Madrid have been struggling at this stage of the UEFA Champions League (UCL) for a while now, but salvation may await when a Viking side who struggle on the road in Europe visit the Spanish capital. 

Match News and Current Form

Atlético bounced back from successive defeats before the international break with a 3-0 win over Getafe on Sunday night to hang on to the coattails of the early La Liga pacesetters. They’re nowhere near the top of the UCL league phase table though, after a slow start to their campaign has seen them pick up four points from the opening four rounds, contributing to a record of just one win in their last six UCL league phase outings (D2, L3). They have at least won ten of the last 13 matches they hosted at this stage (L3), but it's worth noting that one of those defeats came against Norwegian opposition in the form of Bodø/Glimt last season!

Viking lost crucial ground on Bodø/Glimt in the Eliteserien over the weekend, as their 3-2 defeat to Tromsø left them three points adrift at the summit with just three games remaining. They’ll have to quickly park that disappointment if they’re to build on their first-ever win in the UCL proper against Sabah in the last round, but that appears a tall order on the road, where Viking have won just two of their last 13 European games (D4, L7). Two of those defeats came in this season’s league phase, though both did see Viking get on the scoresheet, as they’ve done in a club-record 12 successive European matches.

Head-to-Head History

Prior to last season’s loss against Bodø/Glimt, Atlético had won all six matches against Norwegian opposition. Viking lost their two previous visits to Spain without scoring.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Atlético have only won one of their last seven Wednesday-held UCL matches (D3, L3).

Atlético have only kept one clean sheet in their last ten UCL home games.

Only three clubs took more corners than Viking (28) across the first four UCL rounds.

All four of Viking’s league phase games produced over 3.5 goals.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

After his goal against Getafe, Atlético’s Álex Baena has now opened the scoring with each of his last three goals for club or country, though only one of his last seven strikes came in a home game. Anything positive about Viking may come through Zlatko Tripić, who has been involved in five of Viking's nine UCL goals (G2, A3), while his last 23 club strikes came in games which produced over 2.5 goals.

Cristian Romero missed Atlético’s game on Sunday, while Viking appear to be at full strength.
