FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

After suffering the joint-heaviest defeat by any Manchester United manager in their first game in charge, Mauricio Pochettino returns to the UEFA Champions League (UCL) stage with a tough trip to Sporting CP.

Match News and Current Form

Five successive wins in all competitions - the last two of which came under new manager Paulo Fonseca - mean Sporting return to the UCL in good form as they look to break into the top eight of the league phase table (W3, L1). None of Fonseca’s five permanent predecessors won their first UCL game, but the Lions must fancy their chances of ending that run here given that they’ve won six of their last eight home matches in this competition (L2). It seems almost certain that they’ll be on the scoresheet in any case as Sporting have scored in 22 successive group/league phase matches - an all-time record for a Portuguese team.

No team in Europe look less equipped to deal with Sporting’s attack than Manchester United, who were thrashed 4-0 by Liverpool on Sunday in a chastening touchline debut for Pochettino. The Red Devils are now winless in a negative club record nine Premier League games and have conceded 34 goals in all competitions this season - more than any other side in a top-five European league. United were at least victorious on matchday four of the UCL and sit in the qualification places pre-round (W2, L2), and though their only clean sheet of the campaign came in this competition, they’ve failed to win their last six UCL away matches (D2, L4) and are yet to win a competitive game on the road all season (D3, L5).

Head-to-Head History

Sporting’s only win in four previous H2Hs (L3) came way back in March 1964 (5-0), with United now unbeaten in 13 successive matches against Portuguese opponents (W9, D4).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Sporting are yet to trail at half-time in the UCL this season (HT: W1, D3).

Twelve of Sporting’s last 14 home fixtures produced over 2.5 goals.

United have conceded 2+ goals in their last three UCL games - they’ve never done so in four.

United have received a red card in three of their last four matches.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Sporting goalkeeper Rui Silva starts the matchday with a UCL-high save percentage (85.7), but he’s lost all three of his prior European meetings with United. Opposite number Senne Lammens has been kept busy lately, making 16 saves across his last three appearances for club and country, although he’s also conceded six times in that run.

Sporting seem to have no fresh issues, while United are without the suspended Matheus Cunha.

Betting Analysis

Only pre-round basement dwellers Slovan Bratislava have trailed for longer than United in this season’s UCL, which brings a Sporting half-time/full-time double into consideration.
