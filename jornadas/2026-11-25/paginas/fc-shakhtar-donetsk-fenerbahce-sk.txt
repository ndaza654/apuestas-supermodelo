FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Sitting just inside the play-off positions, Shakhtar Donetsk look to enhance their standing in the UEFA Champions League (UCL) as they take on a Fenerbahçe side high on a historic win in the last round.

Match News and Current Form

Concerns might be brewing over Shakhtar Donetsk’s form, after a 1-1 draw with Livyi Bereh on Friday left them without a win in three games (D2, L1) for the first time since October 2025. That run started with a narrow 1-0 loss to Sporting in this competition and means the Ukrainian side sit on four points at the half-way stage of the league phase (W1, D1, L2). The victory was a 3-1 success over AEK at their UCL home of Stamford Bridge, with the Miners unable to play matches on home soil due to the conflict in their homeland, and those three strikes account for 75% of the goals scored by Arda Turan’s side in the league phase, with only two teams starting this round having netted fewer times.

Fenerbahçe ended their 18-year wait for a victory in the main draw of the UCL when they beat Liverpool 3-2 in their last league phase outing. That victory sparked something of a turnaround in their competitive form (W2, D1), but as they still remain some way off the title pace in the Turkish top flight, they still have much room for improvement. It’s difficult to see their run continuing here as, despite losing only one of their last 11 European matches (W6, D4), Fenerbahçe have failed to win any of their last 12 UCL proper away games (D3, L9).

Head-to-Head History

Shakhtar are unbeaten across two prior H2Hs (W1, D1), while they’re also unbeaten in their 11 matches against Turkish clubs (W10, D1), winning each of their last seven.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Shakhtar haven’t trailed at half-time in this season’s league phase (HT: W1, D3).

Shakhtar have picked up the joint-second most yellow cards in the UCL pre-round (12).

Fenerbahçe have lost all 12 UCL proper away games in which they conceded first.

Fenerbahçe have drawn the most fouls in the UCL pre-round (55).

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Shakhtar’s Marlon Gomes has made a UCL-high 18 tackles this season but has also committed nine fouls along the way - only three players made more in the first four rounds. Mason Greenwood has netted four goals across his last three Fenerbahçe appearances and is unbeaten across the last ten matches in which he has been on target (W7, D3).

Shakhtar have no fresh injury concerns, while it seems likely that Nathan Aké will be missing for Fenerbahçe.

Betting Analysis

Fenerbahçe have long-standing travelling issues in the UCL, and with Shakhtar holding a formidable record against Turkish opponents, backing a Shakhtar win could be the best approach.
