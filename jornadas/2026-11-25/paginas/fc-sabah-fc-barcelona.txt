FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

With their UEFA Champions League (UCL) campaign heading in the wrong direction, Azerbaijan champions Sabah face the unenviable task of needing to take points from five-time UCL kings Barcelona.

Match News and Current Form

Sabah come into this match feeling a little more confident after ending a five-game winless run in all competitions (D2, L3) with a 5-1 domestic league win over Sumqayit on Friday. Valdas Dambrauskas’ men now need to carry that form forward into matchday five after a 4-1 defeat to Viking earlier this month had them placed outside the top-24 at the halfway point of the league phase. Their sole victory (2-0 against Slavia Prague) came at their UEFA home venue, Baku Olympic Stadium, where they’ll be hoping to upset the odds despite the dreadful record that Azerbaijani clubs possess in major European competition against teams from the ‘big five’ leagues (W2, D10, L39).

Barcelona pulled off an outrageous comeback to beat Villarreal 3-2 on Sunday, extending their winning run to four matches in the process. That streak includes a 3-0 success against Aston Villa in the UCL, where the Spanish side are on course to secure automatic qualification for the Round of 16 after three wins in four attempts (L1). Hansi Flick’s men will now attempt to register back-to-back UCL clean sheets for the first time since 2023 and, whilst they haven’t recorded a UCL clean sheet on the road in ten matches, an impressive record of seven wins from eight competitive away fixtures this term (L1) gives reason to suggest they will at least come out on top.

Head-to-Head History

This is the first time Sabah have faced Spanish opposition as well as Barcelona’s first tussle with a club from Azerbaijan.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Sabah have faced at least five shots on target in each of their last seven UCL clashes.

Seven of Sabah’s last eight matches in this competition produced over 2.5 goals.

Barcelona have recorded 8+ shots on target in three of their four UCL games this term.

There have been over 2.5 goals in 14 of Barcelona’s last 16 UCL away fixtures.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Xander Severina’s opening goal in Sabah’s weekend win over Sumqayit means his last seven strikes have served as the first or last of the game. Anthony Gordon scored four goals on his only previous visit to Azerbaijan, a 6-1 win over Qarabağ, while he has won the three matches in which he has scored for Barcelona, with each fixture featuring 3+ goals.

Sabah will be without Aden McCarthy, who was sent off against Viking, while Andreas Christensen is still missing for Barcelona.
