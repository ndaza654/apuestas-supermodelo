A bruised and battered Bayern Munich side look to close in on the UEFA Champions League (UCL) top eight when they travel to a Lille side desperately struggling at Europe’s top table.

Match News and Current Form

A 1-0 Ligue 1 loss to Lyon on Saturday means Lille return to the UCL on the back foot, a familiar feeling in this competition for a side who sat second-bottom after the first four matchdays (D1, L3). A goalless draw with Bodø/Glimt last time out ended a club-record run of five successive European defeats for the Mastiffs, but only once have they ever endured a longer winless streak on the continent than their current six-match slump (D1, L5). Those negative omens follow Lille home as they’ve also lost a club record four successive major European home matches, while they could be beaten in their opening three home fixtures of a UCL campaign for just the second time here.

The previous instance came back in 2012/13 and included a defeat to Bayern, who travel to France after a disappointing 1-1 draw with Cologne on Friday which saw arguably their two best players go down injured. Manager Vincent Kompany was back on the touchline at least, and the Belgian has overseen an unbeaten start to the Bavarians’ UCL campaign (W2, D2), with their only two defeats in this competition since the start of last season coming against 2025/26 finalists PSG and Arsenal (W13, D3). The reversal to PSG was Bayern’s last defeat on the road in any competition (W7, D3), while they’ve scored 2+ goals in 12 of their last 13 UCL games at any venue.

Head-to-Head History

Bayern won both prior H2Hs, which came in the UCL back in 2012. Lille have won just two of their last 11 matches against German opponents in any competition (D3, L6) and have never beaten one at home in the UCL (D1, L2).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Lille have conceded seven goals without reply in the second half of their UCL games this term.

Lille received under 2.5 yellow cards in seven of their last eight competitive home fixtures.

Bayern’s last ten UCL matches have produced 50 goals.

Bayern have scored 11 of their 12 UCL goals this season after half-time.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Lille veteran Olivier Giroud has played a career-high nine UCL matches against Bayern, netting three times along the way, while two of his three strikes this term opened the scoring. Luis Díaz looks set to return here and shoulders great responsibility for Bayern as he makes a 50th appearance in this competition - the last 11 UCL games he scored in produced over 2.5 goals.

Lille’s ex-Bayern defender Tanguy Nianzou was forced off injured at the weekend, a fate that also befell Bayern duo Michael Olise and Harry Kane, with the Englishman set for an extended spell on the sidelines.
