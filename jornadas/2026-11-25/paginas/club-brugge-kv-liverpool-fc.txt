FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

In a repeat of the 1976 UEFA Cup and 1978 European Cup finals, Club Brugge will take on Liverpool with the teams level on points in the UEFA Champions League (UCL) league phase.

Match News and Current Form

Since losing their league phase opener 3-2 to Aston Villa in September, Club Brugge have won ten of their 11 games (D1), culminating in a 5-1 win over Mechelen at the weekend. That run includes their three league phase wins over Inter, Lens, and PSV, with that three-game winning streak being a club record in the UCL proper. That puts the Belgian side in ninth place prior to the round, meaning they’re on track to reach the UCL knockouts for a fourth successive campaign, and an impressive recent record in Wednesday-held UCL home action (W4, D2, L1) is only likely to further fuel belief.  

Liverpool sit a place above their Belgian counterparts pre-round, although their perfect start to the league phase was ended by a 3-2 loss to Fenerbahçe in the last round. The Reds will now look to avoid losing consecutive UCL group stage/league phase games for the first time since November 2018, and they warmed up in perfect style to avoid such a feat as they hammered rivals Manchester United 4-0 on Sunday. That did come at Anfield, and although Andoni Iraola’s men have won five of their last six competitive away matches, three of their last four UCL road trips ended in defeat, so there’s reason for caution here.

Head-to-Head History

Club Brugge have won just one of their last 20 matches against English clubs (D3, L16), while Liverpool have won 13 of their 17 matches against Belgian sides (D2, L2).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Club Brugge’s last 19 UCL proper matches have featured over 2.5 goals, a competition record.

Club Brugge’s last 14 UCL games featured a first-half goal.

Liverpool are the only side to have both scored and conceded 10+ goals in the UCL this season pre-round.

Eight of Liverpool’s last ten away matches featured over 2.5 goals and saw both teams score.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Club Brugge’s Nicolò Tresoldi already possesses the club record for UCL goals in a season with five this term, and he could add to his tally early with five of his last seven in all competitions coming before half-time. He might be beaten to it by Liverpool’s Dominik Szoboszlai, who scored his first goal since returning from injury at the weekend, and has seen three of his last four UCL goals serve as the match opener.

Club Brugge’s Jan Virgili is a doubt with a groin injury, while Liverpool’s Dutch duo Cody Gakpo and Ryan Gravenberch missed the United game and are unlikely to feature.
