FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

PSG have a stunning winning run behind them as they prepare to host a Roma side who have long since struggled on the road in the UEFA Champions League (UCL). 

Match News and Current Form

PSG have made light work of the UCL so far as they go in search of a third successive European crown, winning each of their four league phase outings so far. The alarming ease at which they’ve won those games is evidenced by their 20-goal haul, the most any club has ever managed in the first four matches of a UCL group/league phase, and with the record for an entire such campaign standing at 28, it’s hard to see the Parisians not eclipsing that. A phenomenal 12-match winning competitive run should serve as another confidence booster for them, and with a club-record 14-match unbeaten UCL run behind them (W10, D4), belief is bound to be sky high that they can eclipse Lyon’s French record 14-match undefeated streak in such games here.

Roma might not possess the same length of winning run as PSG, but four successive Serie A victories have kept them firmly in the title hunt. They too have had no issues on the scoring front, rattling in 3+ goals in all four of those wins, with a 3-2 UCL defeat against Manchester United on matchday four sandwiched in between, a result which means Roma have won only one of their last 11 away games in the UCL proper (D2, L8). Conceding in 33 successive UCL road trips certainly hasn’t helped their cause, but salvation could come in a trip to France, as Roma have won on both of their UCL main draw visits to this country.

Head-to-Head History

These sides have never met before. PSG are unbeaten in five home matches against Italian clubs (W3, D2), winning each of the last three, although Roma have never been beaten by French opposition in the UCL proper (W3, D1).

Hot Stats and Streaks

PSG are the only side yet to concede before half-time in this season’s UCL. 

PSG have had at least ten shots on target in all four UCL games this season – no team on record (since 2003/04) has ever done so in five in a row.

Roma conceded seven of their eight UCL goals this term in the second half. 

Roma have won only one of the last 28 UCL away games in which they conceded first (D5, L22).

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

PSG’s Vitinha has featured in all 50 UCL games which Luis Enrique has overseen, and he’s scored or assisted in each of their last three. The referee may need to keep a close eye on Roma’s Gianluca Mancini here, who averages a foul every 25 minutes in this season’s UCL.

PSG’s Désiré Doué is facing months on the sidelines after picking up a knee injury on Saturday, while Roma’s Leonardo Balerdi was also withdrawn with an injury over the weekend.

Betting Analysis

Roma have a scoring knack, but that's unlikely to be enough to beat PSG, so a home win and both teams to score seems a nice combination.
