FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Inter Milan have recovered from a dreadful start to their UEFA Champions League (UCL) campaign and now look to overtake opponents Stuttgart, who have stumbled after winning their opening two fixtures. 

Match News and Current Form

Inter were denied at the death by a late penalty in a thrilling 4-4 draw at Atalanta on Sunday, falling three points behind Serie A leaders Lazio. The Nerazzurri are still in good form, having won six of their last eight competitive games (D1, L1), including back-to-back UCL wins without conceding, which have helped Cristian Chivu’s men bounce back from a two-game losing start in this edition. Inter will expect that positive run to continue here amid a decent record across their last 19 league phase matches which have produced no stalemates (W13, L6), a trend which could continue here as they are yet to draw a competitive match this season as hosts (W7, L1).

Stuttgart come into this game after a goalless draw against Schalke last weekend, although Sebastian Hoeneß’ men have been beaten in just one of their last five competitive games (W3, D1). That defeat came on matchday four of this competition courtesy of a 2-0 loss to Galatasaray, meaning they enter this game winless in two UCL games after winning their first two. Looking to arrest that dip as they bid to qualify for the UCL knockout stage, Stuttgart have a tough job on their hands in a country where they often struggle, albeit they might put up a good fight considering they haven't failed to score in back-to-back UCL games since 2007, while their record in the last eight UCL trips (W4, L4) also suggests we can expect a result either way.

Head-to-Head History

This is the first H2H and while Inter are unbeaten in their last three UCL games against German clubs (W2, D1), Stuttgart have just one win in their last nine away matches against Italian opponents (D2, L6).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Eleven of Inter’s 16 competitive matches this season saw the match-opening goal arrive within the first 20 minutes.

Inter have never lost a home match in the UCL proper when they led at half-time (W29, D3).

Stuttgart’s last eight UCL matches averaged four goals per game.

Only three of Stuttgart’s last 11 competitive matches saw both teams score.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Lautaro Martínez returned from injury at the weekend and could be hoping for a starting role, as seven of his last eight UCL goals have come at home, but both of his strikes this season came in the defeat to Club Brugge. Deniz Undav has been a key figure for Stuttgart in the UCL this season (G1, A2, YC1), with his three goal contributions coming in two wins.

Hakan Çalhanoğlu was forced off injured in Inter’s last match, while Piotr Zieliński missed that game completely. Luca Jaquez was absent from Stuttgart’s game at the weekend.
