FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Braga have an excellent opportunity to record a third consecutive UEFA Conference League game without defeat on Thursday when they host a KuPS side who have won only one competitive match since the start of October. 

Match News and Current Form

After winning only one competitive game from mid-September until the end of October (D1, L5), Braga have found their form in November with four clean sheets contributing to a four-game unbeaten streak (W3, D1). This recent strong defensive showing has also been a feature of their European season, where the Archbishops have registered eight shutouts from nine fixtures, although they have failed to score themselves in four of their last six. Following three consecutive away games, Braga are back at home where they have won two of their last three (L1) and Franclim Carvalho’s side should still be on a high after a 2-0 victory at local rivals Vitória de Guimarães in the Taça de Portugal on Saturday.

By the time this game begins, it will have been 18 days since KuPS last played a competitive fixture due to the completion of their domestic season, giving themselves plenty of time on the training ground to stew over their record of no wins from their last six competitive matches (D3, L3) and only two victories from their last 14 games across all competitions (D5, L7). With only two goals scored, one point on the board and just three teams below them in this competition ahead of this round, Miika Nuutinen’s side could be in for another struggle, particularly as they have not won any of their 14 away games in major European competitions (D4, L10), failing to score in ten of those clashes. 

Head-to-Head History

Braga have never previously faced a Finnish side while KuPS were beaten 1-0 on aggregate by Académica in the 1969/70 European Cup Winners’ Cup, their solitary H2H with a Portuguese team.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Braga have not conceded after half-time in any of their last seven competitive games.

None of Braga’s nine European fixtures this season, qualifiers included, saw both teams score.

KuPS have not scored a second-half goal in any of their last four competitive matches.

KuPS have picked up one booking after half-time in each of their three Conference League games this season.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Ricardo Horta has not scored in Europe since the qualifiers but has five goals from his last five competitive games, opening the scoring for his team in all four scoring matches in that run. Petteri Pennanen looks like one of KuPS’ best hopes of finding the net as three of his last four competitive goal contributions have come in away fixtures (G2, A2).

Both teams appear to have no new availability issues.

For all the latest team news, please check the Flashscore match centre.

Betting Analysis

Both sides have struggled for goals in Europe so backing under 2.5 match goals is the logical approach.
