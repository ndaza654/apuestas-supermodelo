FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Hoffenheim will look to take a step closer to UEFA Europa League (UEL) knockout round qualification and inflict more misery on an out-of-form Sturm Graz side who remain in terrible away European form.

Match News and Current Form

With just one win from their opening four league phase matches (D1, L2), Hoffenheim’s hopes of qualifying for the knockout rounds remain in the balance. Their 1-1 draw away to Crystal Palace on the last matchday may look like a strong return on paper, but conceding a 91st-minute equaliser against ten men left a bitter taste and forms part of a run which has seen them win just one of their last five competitive outings (D1, L3). That sole victory did at least come the last time they played on home soil and they also won their last UEL home match, meaning they are now looking to post successive UEL home wins for the first time since December 2020.

Visiting Sturm Graz have an identical record to Hoffenheim after four rounds (W1, D1, L2) as they look to play knockout European football for the first time since 2023/24. Such a return mainly owes to their goal-shy nature so far, netting just three goals in total in European action which, when coupled with a failure to keep a clean sheet in over a month (D2, L4), makes for an unwanted combination. Snapping that winless run here looks a tall order for a side in the midst of an ongoing nine-match losing streak across away major European fixtures, but they can perhaps cling to the fact their last competitive victory did come in this competition.   

Head-to-Head History

This will be the first meeting between these sides, though Sturm Graz are winless in five away European games against German clubs (D1, L4).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Hoffenheim have kept just two clean sheets across their last 15 competitive matches.

Only one side has won more corners in this season’s UEL than Hoffenheim (31).

Five of Sturm Graz’s last six matches have seen both teams score.

Sturm Graz have faced 5+ shots on target in three of their four UEL games this season.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Fisnik Asllani both scored and assisted in his last home appearance for Hoffenheim, with six of his last eight club strikes now coming on home soil. Oumar Diakité has 12 goals across 13 games for Sturm Graz this season, the last four of which came in losing causes.

Hoffenheim are without a number of first-teamers including midfielder Nathan De Cat, while Sturm Graz are still without first-choice goalkeeper Daniil Khudyakov. 

For all the latest team news, please check the Flashscore match centre. 

Betting Analysis

Given Sturm Graz’s poor recent form and Hoffenheim’s strong home record of late, backing the home win and both teams to score could prove a solid choice.
