FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

An out-of-form Jablonec side will chase their first UEFA Conference League win of this season in the league phase when they host the marauding pre-round table-toppers Lugano.

Match News and Current Form

Jablonec entered matchday four of the league phase in 23rd place after losing their opener and following it up with two stalemates, the most recent of which saw them come from behind to draw 1-1 with Riga. Luboš Kozel’s men are at grave risk of dropping out of the qualification places here having won just one of their last nine matches in all competitions (D3, L5), including none of their last five at home (D3, L2). Even more worrying is the fact they haven’t won any of their last eight matches in major European competition (D5, L3), but Jablonec have a habit of always finding a way to be competitive when hosting overseas visitors, indicated by having not lost a single home game in the Conference League before (W1, D3).

Lugano were flying high at the top of the standings at the start of matchday four, underpinning their success with a free-scoring ruthlessness that has seen them win all three of their league phase matches in fine fashion. The visitors have now scored 3+ goals in each of their last four matches in the Conference League’s main draw (W4), making them the first Swiss side to do so in four consecutive games in the main draw of a European competition. That sets them up to become just the third Swiss team to win five in succession and travel requirements shouldn’t bother them here after they won an impressive 11 of their last 17 away games in all competitions (D5, L1).

Head-to-Head History

Jablonec will compete against a Swiss side for the first time here, while Lugano have won two and lost three of their five outings against Czech opposition.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Just two of Jablonec’s last 12 games were decided by a 2+ goal margin.

Just one of Jablonec’s last six home European matches saw both teams score.

Each of Lugano’s last 13 competitive matches featured over 2.5 goals.

The same half-time result was replicated come full-time in seven of Lugano’s last nine away fixtures.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

After he scored against Riga, Sebastian Nebyla will be feeling as though he is Jablonec’s lucky charm, having lost only one of his last 11 goalscoring appearances for his club (W9, D1). Lugano’s Daniel Dos Santos has shone recently with eight goal contributions in his last eight matches (G5, A3), making his presence felt early having recorded the match opener in four of his last five goalscoring outings. 

First-choice keeper Jan Hanuš remains absent for Jablonec, while Lugano will be without defender Hannes Delcroix.

For all the latest team news, please check the Flashscore match centre.

Betting Analysis

It’s hard to look past backing Lugano to win here as the momentum firmly lies with the visitors.
