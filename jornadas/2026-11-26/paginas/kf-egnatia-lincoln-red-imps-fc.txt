FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

After respectable starts to their UEFA Conference League campaigns, unfancied duo Egnatia and Lincoln Red Imps look to strengthen their qualification bids on matchday four. 

Match News and Current Form

Egnatia opened their first major European campaign with successive draws, but a 2-0 loss to Universitatea Craiova in which they were reduced to ten men on matchday three means they’re outside the top 24. The Albanian side also had a player sent off in a 1-0 domestic defeat on Friday which cut their lead at the summit, so confidence may not be high ahead of this crucial fixture. Egnatia can certainly point to some stellar home form as reason for belief though, as they’re unbeaten on their own turf since April (W10, D3), although all three draws came in Europe. 

Lincoln Red Imps are a point better off than Egnatia (W1, L2), with the latest of those results being a priceless 1-0 victory over Hajduk Split which forms part of a five-match winning streak in all competitions. After losing their first seven major European matches, the Red Imps have now won three of their last eight (D1, L4), with their latest triumph also bringing around their first-ever clean sheet in the main draw of a UEFA competition. Lincoln now look to back that up on the road, but that looks a tall order for a side who’ve secured just one victory from their five European trips this season (D1, L3).

Head-to-Head History

This will be the first-ever meeting between an Albanian and a Gibraltarian side in European competition.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Egnatia failed to win any of the last three European games in which they scored first (D2, L1). 

Egnatia have conceded all four of their league phase goals this term before the 50th minute. 

Eight of Lincoln’s last ten competitive wins were accompanied by a clean sheet.

Seven of Lincoln’s last eight games featured over 2.5 goals. 

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Egnatia’s Alessandro Albanese scored in their last home victory, maintaining his 100% winning record when finding the net for the club (W14). Manu Toledano also boasts a perfect return of six victories when netting for Lincoln, though just one of his 12 strikes therein served to open the scoring. 

Egnatia will be without Eneo Bitri following his red card on matchday three, while Lincoln look to have no new availability issues. 

For all the latest team news, please check the Flashscore match centre.

Betting Analysis

Given Egnatia’s failure to turn recent bright European starts into victories, Egnatia to score first but Lincoln to win seems enticing here.
