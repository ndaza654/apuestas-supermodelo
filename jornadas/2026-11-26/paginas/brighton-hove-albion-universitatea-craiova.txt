FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Surprise Premier League title challengers Brighton look to continue balancing domestic and European commitments when they host a Universitatea Craiova side locked on seven points with the Seagulls in the UEFA Conference League. 

Match News and Current Form

Brighton received a harsh reality check on Sunday as they were beaten 2-0 by Hull, although they do remain Arsenal’s closest challengers at the Premier League summit. Dreams of winning a league title remain some way off yet, but the Seagulls are heavily fancied to win this competition, and it’s easy to see why having lost just twice across their last 14 competitive matches inside 90 minutes (W9, D3). Four successive wins to nil in major European home action only hint at their phenomenal run continuing here, and another such victory would see them become just the third English side this century to win five European home matches in a row without conceding. 

Univ. Craiova maintained their spot at the Romanian Superliga summit on Sunday with a 6-2 win against UTA Arad. The Romanian side are on a superb run of 15 unbeaten matches (W11, D4) and look well-placed to get one of the top-eight Conference League spots following back-to-back wins on matchdays two and three. They haven’t won three in a row in major European competition since a four-game stretch in the UEFA Cup in 1984, but if they’re to achieve that here, they’ll have to snap a six-match streak of alternating between losses and wins in Conference League away matches.

Head-to-Head History

Brighton have never faced Romanian opposition before, while Univ. Craiova won both prior H2Hs against English opposition, beating Leeds home and away in the 1979/80 UEFA Cup.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Brighton are the only side yet to concede in this season’s Conference League.

Only one of Brighton’s last ten home matches saw both teams score. 

Univ. Craiova have scored first in all three league phase games this term.

The two league phase goals Univ. Craiova conceded this season arrived after the 75th minute.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Brighton’s Jack Hinshelwood could be a prime candidate to get on the scoresheet here having netted his last five goals in home games, all of which came in wins to nil. Univ. Craiova’s Ștefan Baiaram netted a brace on Sunday and may fancy his chances again here given his last four goals arrived before half-time in away games. 

Neither side appears to have any fresh concerns for this game.

Betting Analysis

This game represents a huge step-up in quality for the Romanian side, so backing Brighton to win to nil may not be a bad choice.
