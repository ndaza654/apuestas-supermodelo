FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Both may be struggling domestically, but NEC Nijmegen and Rennes are performing admirably in the UEFA Europa League (UEL) and can bolster their qualification chances with a win here. 

Match News and Current Form

Nijmegen’s first appearance in the main draw of a European competition since 2008/09 got off to a rocky start with a heavy defeat at Juventus, but a three-match unbeaten run since (W2, D1) has seen them score seven goals across a pair of impressive wins over Dinamo Zagreb and Omonia. Dick Schreuder’s men have earned all seven of their points from a losing position thus far, but that resilience has largely been absent at home where they’ve won just one of their last 11 games within regulation time (D6, L4). A 4-1 defeat to PSV here on Saturday left NEC in the bottom half of the Eredivisie, placing even greater importance on their European fortunes.

Rennes have made slower progress this UEL campaign, as although they’ve remained unbeaten, three draws have undermined their progress. Losing just two of their 21 major European group/league phase matches since the 2021/22 season (W11, D8) highlights their comfort in this setting, but Franck Haise’s men are chasing improvement after a four-match winless run in all competitions (D2, L2) which has dropped them into mid-table in Ligue 1. They’re especially keen for an upturn on the road where Rennes are winless in six (D3, L3) and have won just once since April.

Head-to-Head History

This is NEC’s first game against a French team, while Rennes have lost just one of their four meetings with Dutch teams (W2, D1).

Hot Stats and Streaks

NEC have been behind at half-time in five of their last six European matches. 

Nine of NEC’s last ten games saw both teams score.

Just two of Rennes’ last ten European matches were decided by a 2+ goal margin.

Four of Rennes’ last five away matches produced over 3.5 goals.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

NEC’s Bryan Linssen is a man in form with three goals in as many games, all of which came after half-time in home games. Boulaye Dia has netted six goals in his last seven games for club and country, while four of his last five for Rennes came in the first half.

Nijmegen defender Perr Schuurs has not featured since going off injured on matchday four, while Rennes’ Valentin Rongier and defender Przemysław Frankowski are unlikely to feature.

Betting Analysis

These two usually deliver entertainment, making the over 3.5 total goals market rather attractive.
