FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Nordsjælland put a strong European home record on the line when they host Panathinaikos, who have a burgeoning reputation on the continental scene. 

Match News and Current Form

Nordsjælland pulled off a shock result in the Danish Superliga on Sunday as they downed league leaders Copenhagen 5-0 at home. That brought a timely end to a winless three-match home run prior for the Danish side, who have tended to do their best work in Europe on home turf, where they’re unbeaten in four matches in major European competition. A failure to keep a clean sheet in the last nine major European matches they hosted should be enough to keep feet firmly on the ground though, as they seek their first win of this league phase cycle (D2, L1). 

Panathinaikos have arguably exceeded expectations in this Conference League this term, posting nine points from their opening three matches (W3), with all three wins bedded within a nine-game winning run in competitive action. In a four-way share of the Conference League lead pre-round and with their spot in the knockouts almost certainly assured, the Greek Super League leaders replicating their run to the last 16 of this competition in 2024/25 is looking increasingly likely. Panathinaikos have been quietly improving on the European scene for quite some time now, considering they’ve lost just one of their last 12 matches in major European competition (W6, D5), as they look to open a continental campaign with four successive victories for the first time since 2007/08. 

Head-to-Head History

Nordsjælland have lost their last four games against Greek opposition, while Panathinaikos have lost three of their five major European matches against teams from Denmark (W2).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Nordsjælland picked up the most yellow cards in the first three league phase rounds (11). 

Nordsjælland scored 2+ goals in five of their last six home games.

Only one side took more corners than Panathinaikos (27) across the opening three rounds.

Panathinaikos’ nine games in European competition (including qualifiers) this season all saw both teams score. 

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Nordsjælland’s Alexander Lind is in sensational form at present, contributing to eight goals in his last two appearances (G4, A4), with three of those goals arriving after the 70th minute. Elmin Rastoder could grow into the game for Panathinaikos having netted his last five goals for club or country in the second half. 

Tobias Salquist was missing in Nordsjælland’s last game, while Panathinaikos’ Santino Andino picked up an injury in their weekend outing.

Betting Analysis

Considering the form Panathinaikos are in on the European circuit, an away win is priced up rather attractively.
