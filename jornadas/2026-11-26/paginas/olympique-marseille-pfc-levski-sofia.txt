FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Marseille are desperate for points in their quest to play knockout football in the UEFA Europa League (UEL), and now tackle a Levski Sofia side who are well placed in the league phase.

Match News and Current Form

Marseille’s four-match winning streak came to an abrupt halt on Sunday night as they let slip a two-goal lead in a remarkable 3-2 defeat to Le Mans. That result will be a worry for manager Bruno Génésio, whose side have now lost four of their last six outings on home soil (W2), with one of those reversals coming at home to Olympiacos in this competition and leaving the French outfit at risk of losing three major continental matches in a row at Stade Vélodrome for the first time since November 2020. They did at least beat Bayer Leverkusen 1-0 in their last UEL outing, but after tasting defeat in their opening three league phase fixtures, they’re already playing catch-up in the race for a top-24 finish.

Having seen a seven-match unbeaten run (W5, D2) come to an abrupt end before the international break, Levski Sofia bounced back to form emphatically by hammering Lokomotiv Sofia 4-1 on Sunday. That result leaves the Blues in second place in the Bulgarian top flight, and things are going pretty well in the UEL too, where two wins from four attempts (D1, L1) leave them well placed to reach the knockout stages of a continental competition for the first time since the 2005/06 UEFA Cup. One of those triumphs was a 2-1 victory at Plzeň earlier this month and means the Blues could now register three European successes on the spin for the first time across the same campaign since 1969.

Head-to-Head History

The only previous meeting between these two teams ended in a 1-0 victory for Levski in the 2005/06 UEFA Cup.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Marseille’s last 12 competitive matches saw both teams receive a yellow card.

Marseille’s last three UEL matches featured exactly one goal.

Only one team has had fewer shots on target than Levski Sofia (10) ahead of this round.

Levski’s four league phase games didn’t produce a single goal in the first 30 minutes.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

No player enters this round having had more shots off target than Marseille’s Igor Paixão (seven), but he could break that streak here with five of his last six club goals coming at home. Eight of Juan Perea’s last ten goals have come after half-time, while each of his five goalscoring appearances for Levski this season ended in victory.

Marseille hope to have Pierre-Emile Højbjerg available after illness, while Levski Sofia have no obvious fresh injury worries.
