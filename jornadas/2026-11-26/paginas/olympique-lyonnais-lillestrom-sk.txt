FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Lyon will hope to continue their impressive home record and move closer to the top eight in the UEFA Europa League (UEL) league phase when a winless Lillestrøm visit Parc Olympique Lyonnais on matchday five.

Match News and Current Form

Lyon won 1-0 against Lille last weekend in Ligue 1, making it a winning start for new manager Carlos Vicens, although that was just their second win across six matches (D1, L3), including winning only one point across the last two UEL matchdays. Those continental results dampened spirits after they started the league phase with back-to-back wins, and restoring that momentum could see Les Gones move into the top eight, as they are behind only on goal difference ahead of this round. A five-match winning run at this venue should boost Vicens’ hopes of not only winning on his home bow, but also extending Lyon’s run of five wins from their last five UEL home games.

Lillestrøm won 2-0 at the weekend in the Eliteserien for a fifth victory in their last six matches (L1). That domestic run masks their deficiencies in Europe, as they remain winless in the league phase (D1, L3) and have spent 264 minutes and 47 seconds trailing in this competition. Hans Erik Ødegaard’s men look to change that script here, arriving on the back of three consecutive away wins, each with a clean sheet, but replicating that on the road looks a big challenge, as the Norwegians have historically struggled in UEFA competitions, with just one win across their last 12 away matches (D5, L6).

Head-to-Head History

These teams have never met in European competition before, and while Lyon are unbeaten in all four UEFA games against Norwegian clubs (W3, D1), Lillestrøm are winless in their five UEFA matches against French opposition (D1, L4).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Lyon have scored 2+ goals in eight of their last ten home UEFA matches.

Seven of the last 11 goals in Lyon’s UEL campaign were scored in the second half.

Seven of Lillestrøm’s last ten competitive goals were scored after half-time.

Only four of Lillestrøm’s last 18 competitive away games saw both teams score.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Only two players have created more chances in the UEL this season than Keito Nakamura (12), and his three direct goal contributions (G1, A2) have featured in two Lyon wins in this competition. Thomas Lehne Olsen could be looking for a late impact as his three UEL goals this season have all arrived beyond the 75th minute, each serving as the last goal of the match.

Lyon avoided any new major injury concerns last weekend, while questions remain over Camil Jebara’s availability for Lillestrøm.
