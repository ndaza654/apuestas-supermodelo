FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

With both still winless after the opening three matchdays, this clash between strugglers Iberia and Getafe looks crucial to both sides’ chances of progression in the UEFA Conference League.

Match News and Current Form

Iberia may be top-of-the-table domestically after a victory at the weekend, but they’re running out of time to extend their first-ever major European campaign. The Red Eagles were beaten 2-1 away at Copenhagen on matchday three to remain outside of the top 24 (D1, L2), but a one-point deficit means one of the competition’s biggest underdogs are still in contention to progress. A first-ever major European victory would go a long way to securing a place in the knockout stages, but this season’s showings indicate that Iberia have very little chance of doing so as they’ve failed to win any of their five European home matches this term inside 90 minutes (D3, L2).

Getafe arrive in Georgia on the back of three successive losses without scoring in all competitions, the first of which came against Brighton last time out in the Conference League. Handling domestic and European commitments has had a detrimental effect on both for the Azulones, who are also struggling towards the bottom of La Liga. Jose Bordalas’ men have lost seven of their nine competitive away games this season (W1, D1) and last won on the road against a top-flight team back in April, but the calibre of the opposition here should give them hope of ending their five-game winless run in major European fixtures (D1, L4).

Head-to-Head History

This will be a first-ever major European meeting between Georgian and Spanish sides.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Iberia’s last 14 competitive home wins came by a one-goal margin.

Only one side received more yellow cards than Iberia (10) across the first three matchdays.

Only one of Getafe’s last six away matches against top-flight opposition saw both teams score.

Getafe failed to score in ten of their last 11 away defeats.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Jemali-Giorgi Jinjolava is bidding to become the first Georgian player to score multiple goals for a side from Georgia in a major European competition in over 20 years. Iván Azón was on target for Getafe on matchday two and has seen each of his last three goals serve as match openers, two of which came beyond the 75th minute.

Iberia look to have no fresh concerns, while Getafe’s Sebastián Boselli missed their weekend outing through injury.

For all the latest team news, please check the Flashscore match centre.

Betting Analysis

A meeting between two strugglers points to under 2.5 goals being a prime option, especially with shot-shy Getafe involved.
