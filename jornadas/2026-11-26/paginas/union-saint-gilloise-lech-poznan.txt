Royale Union Saint-Gilloise are flying high in the UEFA Europa League (UEL), and matchday four sees the Belgian outfit host Polish champions Lech Poznań, who are struggling on the continent.

Match News and Current Form

Royale Union are yet to taste defeat in 90 minutes in any competition this term (W14, D6), leading the way in the Belgian Pro League and faring very well in the UEL (W3, D1). David Hubert’s side have won seven games on the spin, and have already matched the club’s joint-best unbeaten run in the main draw of major European competitions (W4, D1), creating the opportunity to write their names into the history books outright here. Les Unionistes haven't made it out of the UEL group/league phase since the 2022/23 campaign, but look well-placed to achieve automatic qualification for the knockouts this time around.

Lech made it back-to-back domestic title triumphs in 2025/26 and sit top of the Ekstraklasa table again having lost just one league game this term (W11, D2), but haven't maintained anywhere near that form on the continent. Niels Frederiksen’s side collected a point at home against Sunderland (1-1), but have lost their other three UEL matches by at least a two-goal margin and sit second-bottom of the league phase table. The Railwaymen’s fortunes must change swiftly if they are to stand any chance of progressing into the knockouts, but having lost five away games on the spin in the main draw of the UEL, it looks unlikely that will happen here.

Head-to-Head History

This is the pair’s first meeting and Royale Union’s first-ever H2H with a Polish side. Lech are yet to draw in nine H2Hs against Belgian clubs (W4, L5).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Royale Union haven't conceded over 1.5 goals in any of their four UEL games this season.

Royale Union have taken under 4.5 corners in each of this term’s four UEL games.

Eight of Lech’s last 11 competitive games saw both teams score.

Lech have conceded a competition-high three penalties in this season’s UEL league phase.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Besfort Zeneli scored both of Royale Union’s goals as they beat Celta Vigo 2-1 last time out in the UEL, and has registered four goal involvements in their last seven matches (G3, A1). Attempting to keep him out here, Lech goalkeeper Mateusz Lis has made a competition-high 29 saves in this season’s UEL league phase.

Royale Union remain without long-term absentees Kevin Mac Allister and Mateo Biondić. Lech’s lengthy injury list includes João Moutinho and Patrik Wålemark, with a late check needed on team-mate Pablo Rodríguez.

Betting Analysis

The hosts’ form has been remarkable, and if they perform as well as they have been doing in this season’s UEL, Royale Union to win both halves looks a likely outcome.
