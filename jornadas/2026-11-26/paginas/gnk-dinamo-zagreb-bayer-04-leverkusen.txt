FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Two sides well within the fight to directly qualify for the last 16 of the UEFA Europa League (UEL) battle it out in Croatia on matchday five as Dinamo Zagreb host Bayer Leverkusen.

Match News and Current Form

With seven points after four matches, Dinamo Zagreb remain on course to qualify for the knockout stages of this competition for a second successive campaign. The Blues begin this matchday sat just outside the top eight, with their defensive performances seeing them keep three clean sheets and underpinning their success thus far. That’s the same number of shutouts they managed across their prior 24 European fixtures, something the Croatian champions hope to replicate here as they look to win three of their first five group/league phase matches in a European campaign for the first time since 2020/21.

Bayer Leverkusen are also looking to take another step closer to the last 16, and are chasing a response to matchday four’s defeat to Marseille (1-0) which left them three points off the summit. Die Werkself boast the competition's joint-best defensive record having kept three clean sheets prior to that defeat, and a 4-0 rout of Paderborn in the Bundesliga on Saturday ought to have boosted confidence yet further. Four wins in their last five away matches reinforces Leverkusen’s tag as favourites here, as does an eight-game unbeaten run on the road in this competition (W6, D2) which has seen them keep five clean sheets.   

Head-to-Head History

This will be the first meeting between these sides, though Dinamo have lost their last eight European games against German teams.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Six of Dinamo’s last seven major European home matches saw a side score 3+ goals.

Only three teams have picked up fewer UEL yellow cards this season than Dinamo (5).

Six of Leverkusen’s eight UEL goals this term came before half-time.

Leverkusen have registered a joint-high 36 shots on target in this season’s UEL.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Dinamo's Dion Beljo has scored more UEL goals than any other player (9) in the last two seasons, eight of which came on home soil. Leverkusen’s Aleix García has four goal contributions across his last five club games (G1, A3), with each of his last three strikes serving as match openers inside ten minutes.

Dinamo appear to be at full strength for this clash, though Leverkusen remain without Martin Terrier due to injury.

With Dinamo performing well defensively and Leverkusen losing their last UEL outing, the draw market looks an enticing proposition here.
