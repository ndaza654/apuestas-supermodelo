FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Ferencváros will be gunning for top spot in the league phase of the UEFA Europa League (UEL) when they host an improving Celje, who achieved a surprise win in their last overseas trip. 

Match News and Current Form

Heading into matchday five, Ferencváros are one of three teams in this season’s UEL main draw with four wins from four games after a surprise 2-0 triumph at AC Milan, which was one of two victories that they have already secured as underdogs in this league phase campaign. The 36-time Hungarian top-flight champions are now pursuing a fifth consecutive major European main draw win for the first time since May 1968 and have the long-term form to suggest that they should achieve that feat. Their domestic league aspirations took a hit at the weekend when they lost 3-1 to Győr, but with that being their first competitive defeat in 23 matches (W15, D7), and back-to-back losses last coming in February, it could just be an anomaly. 

Celje could be in the mood to cause another upset of their own though after they won 3-2 at Sturm Graz on matchday four, picking up their first-ever UEL league phase victory in the process. That was the second game of an ongoing five-match winning streak for the Counts across all competitions, their longest since an eight-game one between March and April, with a clean sheet at the weekend another promising sign. Shutouts have been especially difficult to come by for Celje in European football this season, as they have earned only one in ten such matches (W3, D3, L4), although they have lost just two of their five away games in that run of results (W1, D2).

Head-to-Head History

Ferencváros have not met a Slovenian team since the country declared independence in 1991, while Celje have never played a side from Hungary.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Ferencváros’ last seven matches were decided by a 2+ goal margin.

Ferencváros’ last ten goals all came after half-time.

Six of Celje’s last nine losses saw them score.

Four of Celje’s last five matches produced over 2.5 goals.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Ferencváros star Kristoffer Zachariassen has been one of the UEL’s standout performers this season, having been directly involved in more goals (G3, A3) than any other player after four matchdays. Benjamin Verbič struck at Sturm for Celje and followed that goal up with one at the weekend, the tenth of his last 12 strikes to arrive after half-time.

Ferencváros’ Bamidele Yusuf is serving a two-game ban after being sent off against Milan, while Celje don't seem to have any fresh absentees.

Betting Analysis

Both teams’ recent games have featured plenty of goals, so over 2.5 goals in this encounter looks pretty likely.
