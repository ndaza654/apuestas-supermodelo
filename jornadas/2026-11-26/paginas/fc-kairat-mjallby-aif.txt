FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Kairat Almaty will still be pursuing their maiden win in the main draw of any major European competition when they host continental debutants Mjällby, who are unbeaten in the UEFA Conference League so far this season. 

Match News and Current Form

Kairat overachieved in a 1-1 draw away to Atalanta on matchday three of the competition but remained winless in 17 European main draw fixtures (D5, L12) and have collected just two points during the league phase of this season’s Conference League. That was the most recent assignment for the out-of-season Kazakhstani side, who can take heart from the fact that they have scored in all three of their league phase matches so far this term after going scoreless in four consecutive Champions or Europa League qualifiers. That phase of their continental journey saw them record the first two of three consecutive continental home defeats, during which they conceded nine goals and scored only one.

Mjällby also ended up in the Conference League after unsuccessful attempts to qualify for the Champions and Europa Leagues earlier this season, but they have since fared considerably better than their upcoming opponents in the Conference League (W2, D1). Their latest win in this league phase - 2-0 over Borac Banja Luka - means that all three of their victories in Europe this term have been accompanied by a clean sheet, complementing the one goal conceded across their last three domestic matches. Mjällby’s next achievement to tick off is their inaugural away win in all European football, something they have not achieved in four attempts (D2, L2).

Head-to-Head History

The only previous meeting between a Kazakhstani and a Swedish team saw Astana beat AIK 4-1 on aggregate in 2014/15 UEL qualifying. 

Hot Stats and Streaks

The last five goals scored or conceded by Kairat in any competition came after half-time. 

Kairat won only one of their last four competitive matches when scoring the opening goal (D3). 

Ten of Mjällby’s last 11 competitive games featured under 2.5 goals. 

Mjällby’s last eight UEFA matches all produced 0-0 half-time scores.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Marc Gual’s match opener for Kairat against Atalanta was the fifth time in his last six scoring appearances that he has netted the club’s first goal of the game. Villiam Granath put Mjällby in front against Malmö on Saturday in a 1-1 draw, recording the seventh successive time that he has avoided being on the losing side when scoring (W5, D2). 

Kairat do not seem to have any fresh absentees, while Mjällby pair Jacob Bergström and Ludvig Tidstrand went off injured against Malmö at the weekend.

Betting Analysis

Given the complete absence of first-half goals in Mjällby’s last eight European games, 0-0 at half-time seems like a wise selection here.
