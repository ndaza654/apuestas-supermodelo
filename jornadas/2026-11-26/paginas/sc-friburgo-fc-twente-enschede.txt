FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

An intriguing UEFA Conference League clash is on the cards when one of Europe’s hottest teams right now Freiburg host a Twente side who haven’t put a foot wrong in the league phase as yet.

Match News and Current Form

After a three-game losing run in October which included a 3-0 defeat against Monaco on matchday two of this season’s league phase, Freiburg have since turned up the heat with five successive wins, with the most recent three being accompanied by clean sheets. In that run is a 1-0 win away to Trabzonspor in their last European outing before they beat Elversberg by the same score at the weekend, also away from home. Returning to the Europa-Park Stadion will only fill them further with confidence, as the Breisgau Brazilians have won their last 12 home games in major European competitions, rendering them just the second German side this century to achieve that feat.

They’ll welcome a worthy opponent in perfect starters Twente, who boast a perfect record after three league phase matches following their 3-1 win over Pafos in the last round, with this being the first time they’ve ever begun a major European campaign with three successive wins. That made it six wins from their seven major European games since the beginning of 2025 (L1), meaning their confidence will be sky high before even considering they picked up another victory when beating Willem II at the weekend and the fact they’re unbeaten in their last eight away matches (W5, D3).

Head-to-Head History

Freiburg have experienced a mixed bag of results in their three encounters with Dutch opposition (W1, D1, L1), while Twente have won just one of their nine European away games against German sides (D3, L5).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Ten of Freiburg’s last 11 European outings featured goals in both halves.

Ten of Freiburg’s 11 prior European matches featured over 2.5 goals.

Nine of Twente’s last 11 European games saw both teams score.

The half-time result was replicated at full-time in five of Twente’s last six away fixtures.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Vincenzo Grifo has seen his last four goals for Freiburg come in Europe, while he’s also netted five of his last six goals at home. Twente’s Daouda Weidmann has recorded a goal involvement in all three of his UEFA Conference League games this season (G2, A2), with both goals contributing to three of his last four strikes coming after the 80th minute.

Freiburg don’t seem to be dealing with many injury concerns at the moment, while Twente were without Thomas van den Belt and Max Bruns at the weekend.

Betting Analysis

There should be plenty of goalmouth action here and so both teams to score and over 2.5 goals seems a highly likely outcome.
