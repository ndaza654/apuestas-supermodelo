Ararat-Armenia travel to Salzburg still looking for their first point in a major European fixture, but Armenian sides shouldering a terrible record against Austrian opposition hints at more misery here.

Match News and Current Form

After going down against Austria Vienna on Sunday, Salzburg have now lost two of their last three competitive outings, leaving them playing catch-up in both the Austrian Bundesliga and UEFA Europa League (UEL). Eating up the ground on the sides above them here is far from a foregone conclusion, as after posting a three-match winless UEL run (D1, L2), Salzburg have now been beaten in 11 of their last 16 matches in this competition (W4, D1). Home seems like as good a place as any to add to that win tally though, as despite going down 4-0 to AC Milan in the last European outing they hosted, they had won four in a row prior, while they also won their last two competitive home matches by an 8-1 aggregate scoreline. 

Despite losing just one of their 13 domestic league games so far this season (W10, D2), including a 2-1 away win on Friday, Ararat-Armenia are the only team in this season’s UEL to have lost all four group stage games. It was the last one which will have hurt the most for Tulipa’s side, who led 2-1 in second-half stoppage time against Torreense and still lost 3-2, putting them on the brink of becoming the first team to concede 3+ goals in their first five major European games since Dukla Banská Bystrica from 1984 to 2004. Defence has long-since been their kryptonite in Europe, as the Armenians have failed to keep a clean sheet in any of their last nine continental away matches and unsurprisingly arrive in Austria without a win in any of their last seven games on the road in Europe (D1, L6).

Head-to-Head History

Armenian sides have lost all five previous European meetings with Austrian teams by an aggregate score of 16-0, which includes Salzburg’s 10-0 success over two legs against Urartu in the 2008/09 UEFA Cup qualifiers.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Only one of Salzburg’s last six European games saw both teams score.

Salzburg have failed to keep a clean sheet in their last seven competitive matches (W4, D1, L2).

Ararat-Armenia have conceded before the 25th minute in their four league phase matches this season.

Only two sides have collected more UEL league phase yellow cards than Ararat-Armenia (13) this season.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Jesper Lindstrøm tends to be in the thick of the action when he’s involved from the off, contributing to at least one goal (G2, A2) or being booked (Y1) in his last four competitive starts. Bruno Wilson has scored two of Ararat-Armenia’s four goals in this competition with both coming after half-time, but another goal could be a negative omen given his last four club goals came in defeats.

Salzburg appear to have no new injury worries, while João Bravim is suspended for Ararat-Armenia.

Betting Analysis

Ararat-Armenia can’t buy a win in the UEL, so backing Salzburg to win and score 2+ goals given their scoring exploits at home seems a smart avenue to go down.
