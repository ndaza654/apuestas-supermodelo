FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Sparta Prague and AZ go to battle for a second successive season, with the hosts in greater need of a strong result in this UEFA Europa League (UEL) clash given their dire form.

Match News and Current Form

A run of one win in nine matches across all competitions (D4, L4) and none in their last five (D2, L3) led Sparta Prague to sack manager Brian Priske and sporting director Tomáš Rosický this week. A whopping 13 points off the title pace domestically, Sparta have also struggled in Europe, failing to win any of their last three league phase games (D1, L2) after emerging victorious on matchday one. One of those defeats came at home to Bournemouth last time out in the UEL, and puts the Czech side at risk of losing successive home matches in this competition for the very first time here.

To inflict that fate, AZ will need to win back-to-back away games in this competition for the first time since December 2006. They were winless in their nine UEL trips prior to matchday three’s 4-0 thrashing of Ararat-Armenia, and have since been beaten by Juventus, although they remain comfortably inside the top 24 (W2, L2). The Cheese Farmers are therefore well placed to match their run to the last 16 of this competition from 2024/25, and confidence will have been boosted by firing in nine goals across a pair of Eredivisie wins since that Juventus defeat.

Head-to-Head History

AZ won both H2Hs in the last 16 of the Conference League last season, including a 4-0 victory on this ground, stretching their unbeaten record in European games against Czech teams to ten matches in the process (W6, D4).

Hot Stats and Streaks

All six of the goals Sparta conceded in the league phase came after half-time.

Just five teams were given more yellow cards across the first four matchdays than Sparta (11).

The team who scored first went on to win all four of AZ’s league phase matches this term.

Five red cards have been shown across AZ’s last seven games in all competitions.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Andrew Irving has directly contributed to seven goals for Sparta this season (G3, A4), with all three of his strikes coming in home wins which saw them net 3+ times overall. After failing to score in his first 18 major European appearances for AZ, Mexx Meerdink has netted in his last three, though only two AZ players have ever done so in four.

Sparta’s Tobias Guddal is suspended, while Hugo Sochůrek and Santiago Eneme missed their weekend defeat. AZ are without Lewis Schouten, Kees Smit and Weslley Patati.
