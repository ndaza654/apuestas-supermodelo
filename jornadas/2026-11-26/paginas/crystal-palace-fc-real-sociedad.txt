FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Two sides locked on five points in the UEFA Europa League (UEL) clash as Crystal Palace and Real Sociedad each try to strengthen their claim to progress deeper in the competition. 

Match News and Current Form

European football is likely a welcome distraction for the Crystal Palace faithful, who have watched their side fall to four straight winless games (D1, L3) under two different managers. Seeking a first win at the second time of asking, new Palace manager Thomas Frank fell to a 3-2 defeat on his debut against Coventry on Saturday, but will now hope to emulate his success as Spurs boss in his last European league phase (W5, D2, L1). Returning to south London should aid his cause, as not only did four of Frank’s five European wins come in home games, but the Eagles are unbeaten in each of their last eight major European games at Selhurst Park since October 2025 (W5, D3). 

Locked on five points with Palace at the lower end of the pre-round qualification picture, Sociedad will make the journey to England sitting pretty in fifth place in La Liga in spite of a 3-2 defeat to Racing Santander on Sunday. Failing to win three of the last four competitive matches is a concern (W1, D1, L2), and it’s far from guaranteed that run will improve here given Pellegrino Matarazzo has won just two of his eight managerial games in the UEL proper (D4, L2). Three of those four career draws came after Matarazzo’s men scored first, so emulating their multi-goal return from their latest UEL trip, a 6-0 win over Lillestrøm, could be crucial.

Head-to-Head History

This is the first meeting between the sides and only Palace’s second encounter against Spanish opponents since beating Rayo Vallecano in the 2025/26 Conference League final. Sociedad have failed to win in all but one of their 11 H2Hs against English sides (D3, L7) after a 2-1 defeat against Bournemouth earlier this campaign. 

Hot Stats and Streaks

Crystal Palace have conceded first in three of their four league phase outings this season. 

Crystal Palace have scored six of their nine UEL goals this term after half-time. 

Seven of Sociedad’s last eight European matches saw both teams score. 

The half-time result was replicated at full-time in three of Sociedad’s four league phase matches.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Daichi Kamada has contributed to 16 goals across his last 25 UEL appearances (G13, A3) and arrives here in solid form for Palace, scoring in two of his last three league phase games. Sergio Gómez has provided a notable threat from left back this season, assisting in La Liga over the weekend while scoring all three of his goals this season in the UEL. 

Adam Wharton is suspended for Palace after his red card in the last round, while Sociedad’s Ander Barrenetxea awaits surgery for his hamstring injury.

Betting Analysis

Sociedad’s concerning return against English sides could continue here, and backing Palace to benefit from a new manager bounce with a home win could prove sensible.
