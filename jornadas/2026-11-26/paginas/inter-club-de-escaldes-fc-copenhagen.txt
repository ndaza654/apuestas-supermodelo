FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Inter Escaldes look to avoid defeat in a major European game for the first time against UEFA Conference League high-flyers Copenhagen, who travel after a galling domestic defeat.

Match News and Current Form

Inter Escaldes may be unbeaten domestically in Andorra so far this season, but their first-ever UEFA main draw appearance has proven a huge challenge. They’re one of just two sides to suffer defeat on each of the opening three matchdays, though they came agonisingly close to posting their first point last time out when they gave up the lead and conceded decisively beyond the 90th minute to lose 2-1 against Red Star Belgrade. If Inter are to finally enjoy success at this level, it’s likely to happen at home where they’ve been beaten just once since March (W9, D2), and though that reversal came in the league phase, it accounts for half of their defeats across their last 11 European home games (W5, D4, L2). 

Copenhagen’s eight-match unbeaten run (W5, D3) was unceremoniously ended on Sunday as they were thrashed 5-0 by Nordsjælland. Manager Bo Svensson will hope that was just a temporary blip given that the Lions have made their best-ever start to a European group/league phase (W2, D1), although they were far from convincing in a 2-1 win over Georgian minnows Iberia on matchday three. While the calibre of their opposition here gives reason for confidence, Copenhagen are also far from bulletproof on the road where they’ve secured just one win in their last five European trips (D3, L1).

Head-to-Head History

These sides have never met previously, though Danish teams have won all six previous meetings with Andorran opponents in Europe, scoring 17 goals while conceding just one.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Just three sides attempted fewer shots than Inter (22) across the first three matchdays.

Just three of Inter’s last nine home games saw both teams score.

Copenhagen have scored four unanswered goals after half-time in the league phase.

Only two sides committed fewer fouls than Copenhagen (28) pre-matchday.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Borja Arellano has scored both of Inter’s goals in the league phase and has netted in a 1-1 domestic draw since, meaning none of his last three scoring appearances ended in victory (D1, L2). Mohamed Elyounoussi has scored or assisted in four of his last six major European appearances (G3, A1), and has seen 14 of his 21 direct goal involvements in Europe come in away matches. 

Inter are without the suspended Domi Berlanga, while Copenhagen expect to be without goalkeeper Diant Ramaj after he picked up an injury at the weekend.

For all the latest team news, please check the Flashscore match centre.

Betting Analysis

This ought to be one of the most one-sided games in this competition, so backing a Copenhagen victory by 2+ goals certainly makes sense.
