FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Beşiktaş have a great opportunity to maintain their top-eight spot in the UEFA Europa League (UEL) when they take on Hapoel Be’er Sheva, whose campaign is seemingly close to being on its last legs, in Batumi, Georgia. 

Match News and Current Form

Beşiktaş are eighth in the UEL table despite failing to win their last two matches - the latest of which was a 3-2 defeat to Celtic - part of a poor run of form in which they won only one of five competitive encounters (D2, L2). Back-to-back Süper Lig victories since then though have been ideal preparation for this fixture in Batumi, which is hosting for security reasons. The Turkish giants come into this game off the back of a four-goal haul at the weekend and will likely be relying heavily again on their attack to compensate for a defensive unit that has kept just one clean sheet in their last 31 UEL main draw matches.

Be’er Sheva will play their first game in more than two weeks as they attempt to keep their slim hopes of progression alive. Now three points off the top 24 and with just one point (D1, L3), they are quickly running out of opportunities to progress beyond the main phase of a continental competition for the first time in four attempts since the 2016/17 season, after losing 2-0 to OFI Crete. It may be in their best interests to exit this competition soon anyway after their domestic league ambitions took a hit earlier this month with a 1-0 loss to Hapoel Tel Aviv, while their UEL main draw away form remains poor, with nine defeats in their last 11 such matches (D2). 

Head-to-Head History

Beşiktaş won both previous H2Hs, with those games being played in the 2016/17 UEL. 

Hot Stats and Streaks

Twelve of Beşiktaş’ last 14 matches produced over 2.5 goals.

Five of Beşiktaş’ last seven wins came by a 2+ goal margin.

Seven of the nine goals in Be’er Sheva’s league phase games this term arrived in the second half.

Be’er Sheva scored in nine of their last 12 European defeats. 

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Junior Olaitan has scored or assisted in all four of his UEL main draw appearances for Beşiktaş and could become just the second African player since 2009/10 to do so in his first five such UEL matches. Amankwah Forson remains Be’er Sheva’s only scorer in this league phase campaign, with six of his last seven goals for any club coming after half-time and six of the seven arriving in wins.

Beşiktaş’ Ümit Akdağ remains suspended, while Be’er Sheva have been without Eliel Peretz since late October due to injury.  

For all the latest team news, please check the Flashscore match centre.

Betting Analysis

Given that they have earned some handsome victories recently, backing Beşiktaş to win by 2+ goals seems sensible here.
