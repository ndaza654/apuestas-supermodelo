FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

A faltering Celta Vigo look to break into the top 24 of the UEFA Europa League (UEL) against a rampant Bournemouth side who fly high in the league phase on the back of an unprecedented run of form. 

Match News and Current Form

After falling to a fourth straight winless La Liga game on Saturday (D2, L2), Celta Vigo look a long way from the side that have posted consecutive top-seven finishes. Reaching at least the quarter-final in their last two UEL appearances, the Sky Blues need to rally in the second half of this league phase after losing three of their opening four matches (W1). Celta’s five defeats in their last six UEL matches (W1) are just one fewer than they suffered in their first 26 games in the competition (W13, D7, L6), and playing at home isn’t a huge boost either, as Claudio Giráldez’s men have won just twice at Balaídos all season (D3, L4). 

Unlike their flailing hosts, Bournemouth secured a club-record ninth consecutive victory at the weekend, beating Nottingham Forest 2-0 to strengthen their European qualification bid. Yet another record beckons for the Cherries here as they could become the first English side to win their first five games in major European competition, one which would strengthen their claim to top the league phase table. Hitting the road shouldn’t faze Marco Rose’s men either as they have triumphed in both of their UEL trips despite failing to keep a clean sheet, including a 2-1 win on Spanish shores against Real Sociedad. 

Head-to-Head History

This is the first meeting between the sides and Celta’s first clash against any English team since 2016/17, though they’re winless in five against such opponents (D1, L4).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Five of the six UEL goals Celta have conceded this term came after half-time. 

Celta are the only side to lose more than one game after scoring first in the UEL this term (L2).

All seven of Bournemouth’s away matches this season produced over 2.5 goals.

Bournemouth’s last ten games featured over 7.5 corners.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Pablo Durán is Celta’s joint-top scorer this season (G4), although his two goals in his last four appearances were both match openers in 2-1 defeats. Rayan has scored three goals from his opening four UEL appearances for Bournemouth, with only one Brazilian bettering that tally in this competition before they turned 21. 

Borja Iglesias remains a doubt for Celta through injury, while Bournemouth’s Juanlu Sánchez remains sidelined.

Betting Analysis

The contrasting form of the sides points directly to a Bournemouth win here, who look a decent price to take the three points.
