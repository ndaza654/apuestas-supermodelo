FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Gent’s wasteful attacking unit will be looking to sharpen up and increase their goal tally when they host a Red Star Belgrade side who have been scoring and conceding heavily in this UEFA Conference League campaign. 

Match News and Current Form

Gent were one of just three clubs to score only one goal across the first three matchdays of this Conference League season, with their sound defending primarily responsible for having earned four points (W1, D1, L1). Gent’s profligacy in front of goal in the competition this season stands out, with their 2% shot conversion rate (one goal from 52 shot attempts) the lowest of any side, so any improvement in their shooting accuracy could net significant returns. Their defending has also underpinned their success at home, where they have lost just one of their last 13 home games in major European competition (W9, D3), with that defeat - 3-0 against Betis in last season’s UEFA Conference League play-offs - the only time they conceded 2+ goals during that span.

Red Star Belgrade joined Gent in the middle of the table with four points, all of which were claimed at home, with only one side ahead of the round seeing more goals in their games (GF 6, GA 6) than the Serbians. Now with a dominant 11-point lead at the top of their domestic league standings, Red Star can afford to devote even more attention to their promising Conference League campaign as they attempt to advance beyond the league phase of a European competition for a second season running. The Serbian giants have now won six of their last ten major European matches (D2, L2) and, despite stumbling away to Lugano in their most recent away trip (L 3-2), can boast 1-0 wins in their three prior major European away games. 

Head-to-Head History

These teams met in the group stage of the 2020/21 Europa League with Red Star winning both contests (2-1 home and 2-0 away). 

Hot Stats and Streaks

Seven of Gent’s last nine competitive matches produced under 2.5 goals. 

Gent kept a clean sheet in seven of their last nine European wins. 

Red Star conceded a first-half goal in only one of their last nine wins in all competitions. 

Seven of Red Star’s last nine European games featured over 2.5 goals.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Abdelkahar Kadri scored Gent’s only goal since their last continental outing and has won all five of his scoring appearances for the club. Osman Bukari has won his last 14 goalscoring games for any club, eight of them spread across two stints at Red Star.  

Mouhamed El Bachir Ngom and Christian Burgess headline Gent’s injuries, whilst Red Star are expected to be without Timi Elšnik, Bamba Dieng and suspended midfielder Aleksandar Katai.
