FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Sunderland aim to build on an upturn in domestic form in this UEFA Europa League (UEL) clash against Jagiellonia, who are fighting to stay in play-off contention and avoid making unwanted history.

Match News and Current Form

Sunderland were unbeaten in Europe this term before losing 2-0 to Dinamo Zagreb on matchday four, but a win here would put them on the brink of reaching at least the knockout play-off stage (W2, D1, L1). They’ve kept back-to-back Premier League clean sheets since that defeat (W1, D1), and the Black Cats’ defensive organisation is especially important in Europe given that they’ve converted a mere 4.8% of their shots in the league phase. Losing just one of their last four competitive home games is more encouraging for Régis Le Bris’ side (W2, D1), although scoring under 1.5 goals in eight of their nine matches at the Stadium of Light this term points to long-term attacking deficiencies. 

Jagiellonia also lost their matchday four game, going down 1-0 to Levski Sofia, but that was their third defeat of the league phase (W1), leaving them outside of the qualification positions. Having lost their last two European games without scoring, Adrian Siemieniec’s side risk suffering three consecutive major European defeats for the first time, while Saturday’s 3-1 league loss to Cracovia hardly helped their preparations. Jaga will also have to cope without their travelling supporters following a UEFA ban as they try to avoid losing three successive away games in Europe for the first time.

Head-to-Head History

These two sides have never met, but Sunderland have already played Polish opponents in the league phase, drawing 1-1 at Lech Poznań on matchday three. Jagiellonia have never played an English side, but are unbeaten against British opposition (W1, D1).

Hot Stats and Streaks

There were fewer than ten corners in each of Sunderland’s last eight fixtures in all competitions.

Eight of Sunderland’s nine home games this season produced under 2.5 goals.

Three Jagiellonia players were booked in all four of their league phase games this term.

All four of Jagiellonia’s away UEL games this term produced exactly one second-half goal.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Trai Hume has been booked in two of Sunderland’s last three matches, while he received a yellow card between the 20th and 60th minute in three of their four UEL games this term. Nik Prelec’s goal for Jagiellonia against Cracovia on Saturday was a third in his last five appearances, with each of his last four strikes coming in games producing at least four total goals.

Sunderland remain without Dan Ballard, Brian Brobbey and Granit Xhaka, while Jagiellonia don’t appear to have any fresh injury concerns.

For all the latest team news, please check the Flashscore match centre.

Betting Analysis

Sunderland’s poor conversion rate despite creating several chances in Europe, combined with Jagiellonia’s struggles in the league phase, make backing under 2.5 goals in this one a viable choice.
