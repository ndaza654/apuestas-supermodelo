FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Hajduk Split host unbeaten St. Truiden with both sides hoping to boost their chances of automatically qualifying for the UEFA Conference League last 16.

Match News and Current Form

Hajduk Split’s promising start to the league stage took an unexpected turn when they lost 1-0 to Gibraltar’s Lincoln Red Imps on matchday three, although they remain well placed to qualify (W2, L1). The Whites beat Lokomotiva Zagreb 2-1 on Saturday to maintain a four-point lead over rivals Dinamo Zagreb atop the Croatian top-flight, and they now aim for more than two wins in one major European campaign for the first time since earning five in 1985/86. Gonzalo García’s side are also unbeaten across all 12 competitive home fixtures this term (W10, D2) - including a famous 2-1 win over Ajax - suggesting they’ll take some beating here despite this game being played behind closed doors due to UEFA sanctions against Hajduk.

St. Truiden are one point better off than their hosts (W2, D1), conceding just one goal during an impressive maiden major European campaign. Struggling to match that defensive resilience elsewhere cost Frédéric De Meyer his job earlier this month, with Jonas De Roeck back for a second spell as head coach as the Canaries sit an underwhelming 11th in the Belgian Pro League. They’ve conceded an average of two goals per domestic league game this season, but could instead take heart from winning away at Midtjylland on matchday two (1-0) and drawing 2-2 at Charleroi on Saturday in their most recent trip.

Head-to-Head History

This will be the first-ever H2H. Hajduk have won five of their six home European games against Belgian opposition (L1), while St. Truiden have never previously faced Croatian opponents.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Only Thun (68) committed more fouls across the first three matchdays than Hajduk (59).

Four of Hajduk’s five home European games this term produced at least two second-half goals.

Four of St. Truiden’s five European games this term saw at least one goal after the 75th minute.

St. Truiden conceded 2+ goals in four of their last five away fixtures.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Michele Šego has six goal contributions (G5, A1) across Hajduk’s Conference League campaign this season and has opened the scoring in six of his nine goalscoring appearances this term. Ilias Sebaoui has netted in both of St. Truiden’s last two fixtures, with three of his last four goals arriving after the 50th minute.

Abdoulie Sanyang and Marin Šotiček missed Hajduk’s weekend win but may feature here, while St. Truiden do not appear to have any major injury worries.

For all the latest team news, please check the Flashscore match centre.

Betting Analysis

Hajduk’s superb home record in all competitions this term could be too much for a St. Truiden side under new management, so backing Hajduk to win holds appeal.
