FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Brann will be looking to continue their finest European season in more than 50 years by denying Aarhus their first league phase win of this UEFA Conference League season. 

Match News and Current Form

Brann are almost certainly now out of contention to qualify for European football next season via the 2026 Norwegian Eliteserien and can now turn the majority of their attention to what has so far been an excellent Conference League campaign. They began matchday four in sixth place as one of ten clubs yet to be defeated, with their two wins and one draw their best start to any major European competition since the 1973/74 Cup Winners’ Cup. That includes a win over Lincoln Red Imps in their only league phase game at home this season, forming part of a record of seven wins from their last ten home fixtures (L3) in all competitions. 

Goal difference was the only thing keeping winless Aarhus (D1, L2) inside the top-24 at the halfway point of the league phase. They’re at a fascinating part of their season, having completed two-thirds of their Danish Superliga campaign and teetering between the championship and relegation groups in the table. A run of just two competitive defeats since the middle of September (W6, D4) affords some optimism here, but both defeats did come on the road, where they have won only two of their last 12 games in all competitions (D3, L7) and lost eight of their nine continental games played this century (W1).

Head-to-Head History

This is Brann’s second meeting with Danish opposition after equalising three times in a 3-3 draw with Midtjylland in last season’s UEL. Aarhus last met a Norwegian side in the 1960/61 European Cup. 

Hot Stats and Streaks

Seven of Brann’s last nine games in all competitions produced over 2.5 goals.

Eight of Brann’s nine continental games this season saw both teams score. 

Five of Aarhus’ last six competitive matches saw the winning team score exactly two goals.

Eight of Aarhus’ 11 continental defeats this century were by a 2+ goal margin. 

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Kristall Máni Ingason continues to be in stellar form for Brann, scoring or assisting in each of his last six club games (G4, A2), whilst six of his last seven club goals came after half-time. Jacob Andersen is primed to record his maiden continental goal after striking in each of Aarhus’ two wins since their last Conference League match.

Jacob Sørensen leads Brann’s injury list, whilst Aarhus were missing Frederik Tingager and Janni Serra at the weekend.

For all the latest team news, please check the Flashscore match centre.

Betting Analysis

With these sides conceding only five goals combined in six league phase matches, this clash appears ripe for under 2.5 goals.
