FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Atalanta’s miserable European away form in 2026 could get worse when they visit Borac Banja Luka’s fortress in the UEFA Conference League on Thursday.

Match News and Current Form

Borac warmed up for this fixture by scoring four goals in a domestic league game for the second successive match, defeating Željezničar 4-0 away. Vinko Marinović’s men now need to bring that impressive form into the Conference League, having lost two of their opening three league-phase games (W1) this season. That solitary win came against KuPS at home, where Borac have not lost across all competitions since last December, and they could now become just the second team outside Europe's top five leagues to remain unbeaten in their first ten main-draw matches as hosts in major European competition (W6, D3).

Atalanta have not travelled well in Europe recently, losing all four such away games this calendar year excluding qualifiers - their longest streak of main-draw defeats on the road in major continental competition. Their overall form is also disappointing, having won just one of their last eight competitive matches (D4, L3), while they find themselves 16th in the Conference League ahead of this round of fixtures (W1, D1, L1). Avoiding defeat in Bosnia and Herzegovina would see La Dea go four competitive games unbeaten for only the second time since Maurizio Sarri’s appointment in June though, and they will take confidence from their 4-4 draw on Sunday against Inter Milan, achieved despite trailing by two goals on three occasions.

Head-to-Head History

Since their country gained independence, a Bosnian side have yet to beat Italian opposition (D1, L3), while Atalanta’s only previous meeting with a Bosnian team saw them thump Sarajevo 10-2 on aggregate in 2018.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Borac have received at least one first-half yellow card in all three of their league-phase games this season.

Borac kept a clean sheet or conceded only one goal in 14 of their last 15 European home matches.

The half-time result was replicated at full-time in just two of Atalanta’s last seven games.

Atalanta have had more corners than their opponents in their three league-phase matches this season.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Amer Hiroš has been booked in two of Borac’s three league-phase games this term and has registered four goal contributions across their last three Bosnian top-flight matches (G2, A2). Charles De Ketelaere is also in excellent form, with five goal contributions to his name (G3, A2) in his last three games for club or country.

Branimir Cipetić, Anderson Esiti, Ante Roguljić and Stefan Savić could all miss out for Borac, while Atalanta will be without goalkeeper Marco Carnesecchi.

Betting Analysis

Considering the two sides’ respective recent European home and away records, backing a Borac win or a draw as a double chance seems sensible here.
