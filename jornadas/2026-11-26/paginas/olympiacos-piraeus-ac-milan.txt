FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Olympiacos will be looking to maintain their unbeaten record in the UEFA Europa League (UEL) this season as they face an AC Milan side at the wrong end of the table. 

Match News and Current Form

Olympiacos’ strong recent UEL form has continued this season (W3, D1), with the Greek giants now unbeaten in their last 11 such league phase games (W7, D4), conceding just twice in the process. They are also unbeaten in their last 12 competitive matches (W10, D2) under current manager Imanol Alguacil, with their latest outing seeing them thump Iraklis 6-2 on Sunday in domestic action. Their last UEL game against Rennes (0-0) did see the Red-Whites’ 100% main-draw record this term come to an end though, so they will be happy to be returning to the Karaiskakis Stadium, where they have won their last four home matches in the competition. 

Milan also shone at the weekend, thrashing Frosinone 5-1, but unlike Olympiacos, they have struggled in the UEL so far this season, losing three of their four such games (W1). They therefore find themselves 28th in the table, with the Rossoneri now under pressure to avoid suffering three successive defeats in major European competition without scoring for the first time ever, following 3-0 and 2-0 losses to Bournemouth and Ferencváros respectively. That result against the Cherries is Milan’s only competitive away defeat to date under Ruben Amorim though (W4, D3), raising hopes among supporters that their team can get something here. 

Head-to-Head History

Olympiacos have only won one of the previous four H2Hs (D1, L2), but that victory came in their most recent meeting with Milan in the UEL in December 2018 (3-1). 

Hot Stats and Streaks

Olympiacos’ last seven European games were decided by a one-goal margin at most. 

None of Olympiacos’ last 14 UEL matches featured over 3.5 goals. 

Milan haven't drawn any of their last nine games (W6, L3). 

Milan scored exactly two goals before the half-hour mark in four of their last five matches. 

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Jota Silva has had the most shots of any player in this season’s UEL main draw, but hasn't scored with any of those attempts, getting just two on target. Alphadjo Cisse is enjoying a superb breakthrough campaign with Milan, with the 20-year-old’s strike against Frosinone meaning that his last seven goals have all come before the hour mark, a run which includes his hat-trick against Salzburg (4-0) in the team’s only UEL win so far this term. 

After scoring twice at the weekend, Olympiacos’ Leon Bailey then went off injured, while Milan seemingly have no fresh absentees.

Betting Analysis

Given Olympiacos’ strong home form, backing the hosts to win combined with under 3.5 goals is tempting here.
