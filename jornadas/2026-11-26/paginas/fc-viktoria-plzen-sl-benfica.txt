FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Benfica look to maintain their perfect start to their UEFA Europa League (UEL) campaign as they head to Viktoria Plzeň, who are trying to build on their first league phase victory of the season.

Match News and Current Form

Plzeň started the league phase with three straight defeats before beating Lillestrøm 1-0 on matchday four to revitalise their qualification ambitions. Although Radoslav Kováč’s side are unbeaten across their last five competitive fixtures (W4, D1), their struggles at the Doosan Arena do not bode well as they’ve won just one of their last seven home games in all competitions (D2, L4). That includes both league phase fixtures here this term, while winning none of their last six UEL home matches (D4, L2) represents their longest such sequence in major European competition.

Benfica arrive in the Czech Republic hoping to put Plzeň under more pressure, as they sit on top of the league phase standings with a perfect record (W4) having spent 87% of their campaign in the lead - a comfortably higher ratio than any other side. That dominance has become second nature for Marco Silva’s side, who’ve won each of their last nine matches as they try to keep pace with Portuguese top-flight leaders Porto. The Eagles can also take plenty of encouragement from only losing two of their last nine away UEL proper games (W4, D3).

Head-to-Head History

This is the first meeting between these two clubs. Plzeň have lost just one of their previous five UEL meetings with Portuguese sides (W2, D2), while Benfica are facing Czech opposition for the first time since March 1992.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Plzeň conceded first in four of their six UEL games this term, including three of their four league phase fixtures.

Nine of Plzeň’s 11 home fixtures this term saw both teams score.

Benfica led at half-time in five of their last six European fixtures.

Benfica have scored at least twice in each of their last four away games.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Prince Adu has scored twice across his last three Plzeň appearances, including the winner against Lillestrøm, with each of his last five goals in Europe arriving before half-time. Benfica’s Gianluca Prestianni has four goal contributions in his last three UEL outings (G2, A2), while eight of his last ten scoring appearances at club level came in away matches.

Plzeň look to have no new injury concerns, while Fredrik Aursnes is doubtful and Leandro Barreiro is unavailable for Benfica.

Betting Analysis

As Benfica have won all four of their UEL league-phase games by at least two goals, there could be value in backing Benfica -1.5 in the handicap market.
