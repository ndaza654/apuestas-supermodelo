FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Hearts hope an extraordinary league win at the weekend can inspire a European revival as they host a so far perfect Monaco side on matchday four of the UEFA Conference League.

Match News and Current Form

Hearts’ 8-0 win over Dundee on Saturday was their biggest top-flight victory since 1957, ending a three-match losing streak in spectacular fashion. They’ve picked up just one Conference League point so far, but riding high on that remarkable result, Wouter Vrancken’s side will also come into this clash at Tynecastle Park encouraged by their improving home form in Europe. Hearts have lost just one of their last five major European fixtures here (W2, D2), a huge step up from losing each of their previous five without scoring.

Monaco are one of four sides to have opened this Conference League league phase with three successive wins, and victory here would see them win four major European games in a row for the first time since 1997. The Monégasques’ European consistency contrasts sharply with their struggles domestically as Filipe Luís’ side have failed to win any of their last six Ligue 1 games (D4, L2). Claiming a late 1-1 draw against Auxerre on Saturday showed their fighting spirit, which might offer them hope of boosting their record of winning just two of their last five European away fixtures (D2, L1).

Head-to-Head History

These two sides have never met previously. Hearts have one win from four previous meetings with French clubs (D1, L2), while Monaco are unbeaten in their last three visits to Scotland (W1, D2).

Hot Stats and Streaks

There was at least one goal in each half in all five of Hearts’ Conference League fixtures this term.

Seven of Hearts’ last nine home games produced over 2.5 goals and saw both teams score.

Just one Monaco player was booked across their last three fixtures.

Monaco scored at least twice in four of their last five European away games.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Cláudio Braga has scored in five of his last six Hearts appearances, with six of his nine goals this term coming at home. Monaco’s Mathys Detourbet has scored three of his last four club goals between the tenth minute and half-time, including one on matchday three, a game in which he became the youngest Frenchman to score and assist in a major European game since Kylian Mbappé in October 2017.

Hearts have injury concerns over Stephen Kingsley and Tómas Magnússon. Aleksandr Golovin and Christian Mawissa missed Monaco’s Saturday game with injuries.

If Hearts can build on their solid recent home form to pose Monaco some problems, then backing Hearts/Draw in the double chance market might be worth considering.
