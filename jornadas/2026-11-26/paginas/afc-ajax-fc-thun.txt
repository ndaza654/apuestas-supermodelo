FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Ajax are in blistering form at present and look to take the next step on the road to knockout football in the UEFA Conference League when they host a Thun side hoping a win in the last round can help them turn a corner. 

Match News and Current Form

Ajax suffered a shock Conference League matchday one defeat to Hajduk Split (2-1), but that is their only loss in 12 competitive games (W8, D3), having responded with wins over Atalanta (2-0) and Midtjylland (6-1) in this competition. Having scored six goals in a European encounter for the first time this century earlier this month, the Dutch side are unsurprisingly overwhelming favourites to continue their 100% continental home record this season (W4) in this fixture. Tightening up defensively is a priority though for the Amsterdam outfit, who have kept just one clean sheet in their last 20 European matches no matter the venue. 

Thun clinched a first Swiss Super League title last term, but summer appointment Gian-Luca Privitelli has failed to impress after being tasked with overseeing the club’s first-ever Conference League campaign. Privitelli’s side have struggled desperately in domestic action, losing eight of their 14 league games (W4, D2), as well as their first two matches in this competition against Twente and Hajduk (both 1-0). A 3-1 win over Hearts earlier this month did end Thun’s seven-game main draw losing run in Europe and has given them some hope of finishing in the top 24, but they have never won back-to-back matches on the continent in a group/league phase.

Head-to-Head History

Ajax won both previous H2Hs by a two-goal margin, with those two meetings taking place in the 2005/06 UEFA Champions League group phase.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Eleven of Ajax’s last 12 European matches saw both teams score and featured over 2.5 goals.

Ajax have received just three yellow cards in the main draw of the Conference League this term.

Thun’s last five games each saw both teams score and featured over 3.5 goals.

Thun have committed a competition-high 68 main draw fouls this season.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Three of Owen Wijndal’s four goal involvements for Ajax this term have come in the Conference League (G2, A1), including a goal and an assist against Midtjylland. Three of Nils Reichmuth’s four yellow cards this season have come in Europe, with the 24-year-old receiving a booking in both of Thun’s last two games in this competition.

Ajax might be without Viktor Tsygankov, while Caio Henrique and Jorthy Mokio may also not feature here due to injury. Neither Mattias Käit nor Furkan Dursun were in Thun’s squad for their 3-2 loss to Young Boys at the weekend. 

Betting Analysis

Ajax have shown that they are capable of taking teams apart, and if they are in the mood again here, over 3.5 home goals seems realistic.
