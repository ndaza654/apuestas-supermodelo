FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

UEFA Conference League underperformers Pafos and Midtjylland clash on matchday four with both still awaiting their first win of this season’s league phase.

Match News and Current Form

Founded in 2014, Pafos’ third successive campaign in the main draw of a European competition hasn't gone to plan, collecting just one point from their opening three league phase games. For a side who reached the Conference League last 16 in 2024/25 and fought valiantly in the Champions League last term, that’s a disappointing return, although they have at least secured back-to-back domestic victories to end a six-match winless run across all competitions (D2, L4). Ricardo Sá Pinto’s side should fancy their chances at home too, having lost just one of their last nine group/league phase home matches in Europe (W6, D2), and that came against continental heavyweights Bayern Munich.

Danish Superliga table-toppers Midtjylland boast far more European experience than Pafos, but are yet to impress in their first-ever league phase appearance in this competition (D1, L2). The Wolves are hunting a response after a chastening 6-1 defeat against Ajax on matchday three which was their joint-heaviest European loss ever. That came on home turf, and hitting the road may be a blessing for Mike Tullberg’s side, who have won eight of their last 12 European away games (D2, L2) and were last beaten on their travels in any competition back in July (W8, D4).

Head-to-Head History

This is the pair’s first meeting, and Pafos’ first-ever H2H with a Danish side. Midtjylland have won only two of their eight games against Cypriot clubs (D3, L3).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Pafos’ last six European games saw both teams score.

Pafos received a joint-low two yellow cards across the first three matchdays.

Four of Midtjylland’s last eight European games have produced exactly two goals.

Midtjylland have conceded five times without reply in the second half of their league phase games this term.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Ken Sema opened the scoring for Pafos at the weekend, and his other two goal involvements this term have come in Conference League home games (G1, A1). Sofus Johannesen has scored three times in Midtjylland’s last five games, while all five of his strikes this season came after half-time.

Pafos’ Lelê and Midtjylland’s Kjell Wätjen are both banned following red cards. The Danes also have a lengthy injury list including first-choice goalkeeper Nordin Bakker and Denil Castillo.

Betting Analysis

This game looks set to be pivotal for both teams' chances of reaching the knockout stages, so anticipating a slow burner, both teams to score in the second half appears viable.
