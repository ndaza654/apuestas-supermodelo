FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Second-tier Portuguese side Torreense have defied the odds in this season’s UEFA Europa League (UEL) and will look to continue their impressive campaign against a Celtic side who picked up their first win in the last round.

Match News and Current Form

Few expected much from Torreense in the UEL aside from them making up the numbers, but two wins from their first four league phase outings (L2) have placed them firmly in top-24 contention. A dramatic 3-2 victory over Ararat-Armenia on matchday four has been followed up by back-to-back domestic defeats, the first of which left them close to the relegation zone in Liga Portugal 2, while Sunday’s defeat to Benfica ended their Taça de Portugal title defence. Playing away from their home ground due to their stadium not meeting UEFA regulations could be deemed a hindrance here, but having won just two of the eight matches they’ve hosted at any venue this term (D2, L4), that may not play a big role. 

After opening their campaign with three successive defeats, Celtic picked up their first UEL victory when they beat Beşiktaş 3-2 in the last round. The Bhoys are still a point adrift of the top 24 pre-round, but they’re clear at the top of the Scottish Premiership following Saturday’s 4-1 win against St Mirren. It's easy to see why they’re struggling in Europe as their 11 goals conceded is one of the highest such tallies in the league phase, and that’s been an ongoing trend for Celtic on the European scene, as since the start of 2020/21 they’ve conceded more goals in major European competition than any other side (116).

Head-to-Head History

Portuguese teams are unbeaten in their last five UEL matches against Scottish opposition (W3, D2), while Celtic haven’t won away to Portuguese opposition since April 2003 (L5).

Hot Stats and Streaks

All four of Torreense’s league phase games produced over 2.5 goals.

Torreense have conceded seven of their nine goals in the league phase after half-time.

Celtic have scored five of their six league phase goals this term in the second half. 

Celtic haven’t led a league phase game at half-time this season (D2, L2).

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Torreense’s Musa Drammeh has been amongst the goals lately, netting at the weekend and also their late winner against Ararat-Armenia - 11 of his last 12 club goals arrived after half-time. Camilo Durán was Celtic’s hero against Beşiktaş and has a knack for scoring big goals as he’s netted the decisive goal on six of his last nine scoring appearances. 

Adama Baradji remains sidelined for Torreense, while Celtic’s Mika Baur missed their weekend win against St Mirren.

Betting Analysis

Picking a winner is tough here, but second-half trends of these sides hint that backing Celtic to score after half-time could be a good approach to take.
