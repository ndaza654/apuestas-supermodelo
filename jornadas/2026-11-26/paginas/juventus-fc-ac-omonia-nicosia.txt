FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Juventus will want to cement their place in the top eight of this season’s UEFA Europa League (UEL) league phase when they host an Omonia side who are chasing progression themselves. 

Match News and Current Form

Juventus entered this round of the league phase in fifth place after three wins and a draw from their four matches, culminating in a 2-1 win away to AZ Alkmaar last round. Since then they hit the road again in a 2-2 draw with Fiorentina before emphatically beating Venezia 4-0 at home at the weekend in what would’ve been a confidence boosting performance. That result means the Old Lady have won three of their last four matches (D1) and their last two at the Allianz Stadium without conceding, meaning optimism will be high they can extend their unbeaten record at home in the UEL (W6, D9).

After naming the oldest starting XI on average in the UEL this season (29 years, 347 days) with at least six players aged 30+ in all four lineups, Omonia’s experienced squad is unlikely to be intimidated in this match against one of the European greats. That experience seemed to have handsomely paid off when the Cypriots won their opening two league phase matches, but subsequent 4-1 and 3-1 losses to Benfica and Nijmegen respectively see them enter this round in 18th place. Despite their two recent setbacks, manager Henning Berg seems to have instilled an edge of fearlessness into Omonia, who’ve gone out and scored in nine of their last 11 European matches regardless of location. 

Head-to-Head History

These sides have met four times in the past which contributes to Juventus’ 100% winning record against Cypriot teams (W7) and Omonia’s 100% losing rate against Italian sides (L6).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Juventus’ last six European matches featured over 2.5 goals.

Just one of Juve’s last six home games saw both teams score.

None of Omonia’s last eight European matches saw them trail at half-time.

Omonia’s last six away fixtures were decided by a 2+ goal margin.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Juventus’ Nick Woltemade has either scored or assisted in all four UEL games this season (G3, A2), and he’ll be confident he can get on the scoresheet again since four of his last six goals came at home. Omonia’s best route to goal seems to be through Loïs Diony who has scored in his last four matches, with all four goals arriving in the opening 25 minutes and serving as the match opener. 

Kerim Alajbegović went down for Juve at the weekend, joining Pierre Kalulu as the latest additions to their injury list, while Omonia seemingly have no new injuries to contend with.

Betting Analysis

The hosts should prevail but it’ll prove difficult to keep Omonia quiet, hence a Juventus win with both teams to score makes a lot of sense.
