FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Lithuanian Toplyga champions Kauno Žalgiris will continue searching for their first points in the league phase of the UEFA Conference League when they take on Riga, who are looking for a new direction after parting ways with their longest-serving head coach.

Match News and Current Form

Kauno Žalgiris’ 4-1 loss to Transinvest earlier this month was inconsequential as they had already successfully defended their domestic title, but a fourth defeat in their last seven matches (W2, D1) could be of some concern. The hosts have had more than two weeks off to work out how to mount an unlikely European revival as one of two sides to have lost all three of their Conference League matches this edition, during what is their debut in a main draw of any major UEFA competition. Their slim hopes of reaching the knockout phase could depend on winning every remaining league phase game, and they will need a marked improvement on their home record to achieve that, having won just two of their last seven UEFA home games (D2, L3).

Losing the domestic title and then the Latvian Cup final proved to be the last straw for Riga’s Adrián Guľa, who left the club just four days before this fixture. Guľa led them to their own historic league phase debut and the visitors are also still searching for their maiden win after three 1-1 draws in the league phase. The Latvians are unbeaten across their last ten matches in Europe including qualifiers (W5, D5), but have struggled to replicate that form away from home. Riga have won only one of their last 13 away UEFA matches including qualifiers (D4, L8) although five victories from their last eight competitive trips (D2, L1) gives them hope of winning full points here.

Head-to-Head History

This will be the first ever major UEFA match between Lithuanian and Latvian clubs.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Thirteen of the last 18 goals scored in Kauno Žalgiris’ matches have come in the first half.

Kauno Žalgiris conceded 2+ goals in six of their last seven UEFA matches.

Six of Riga’s last seven UEFA matches produced under 2.5 total goals.

Riga conceded exactly two or three goals in eight of their last 11 away UEFA matches.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Amine Benchaib could be the one driving Kauno Žalgiris’ revival in this competition, as he has scored 11 of his last 13 club goals at home. Iago Siqueira could spearhead Riga’s response with a late strike, as five of his last six club goals have come beyond the hour mark.

Both teams reportedly have no new major injury absences ahead of this matchday.

Betting Analysis

Neither team has shown enough bite recently, so backing a draw could prove to be a handy choice.
