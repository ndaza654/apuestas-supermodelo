FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

CSKA Sofia’s prospects of extending their UEFA Conference League campaign will dwindle further if they cannot beat a Trabzonspor outfit who still harbour top-eight ambitions.

Match News and Current Form

After a rollercoaster 3-3 draw at Botev Plovdiv at the weekend, CSKA Sofia have now lost just twice in 15 outings (W8, D5), but both of those defeats have come in this competition. Picking up just a solitary point across the first three matchdays, the Reds have managed only two victories from their last 32 major European matches (D8, L22) and sit outside the top-24 as a result. They can at least draw inspiration from being back at their newly reconstructed Stadion Balgarska Armia home, where they returned with a 0-0 draw against Slavia Sofia last month, which looks timely given that they’ve lost their last three European matches as the designated hosts.

Trabzonspor’s 1-0 victory over Eyüpspor on Sunday was a ninth win from ten matches across all competitions (L1), although that defeat came on matchday three against Freiburg (1-0). Thomas Reis’ men won their two prior league phase games to occupy a play-off place at the halfway stage, with one of those victories coming away at KuPS on matchday one. That ended a run of nine successive away defeats in major European matches, while they could now secure back-to-back wins on their continental travels for the first time since 2013.

Head-to-Head History

CSKA Sofia have lost five of their previous seven clashes with Turkish teams (W1, D1), whereas Trabzonspor are facing Bulgarian opposition for the first time.

Hot Stats and Streaks

CSKA Sofia have registered at least five shots on target in each of their three league phase games.

Each of CSKA Sofia’s three league phase matches saw both teams score and featured over 2.5 goals.

Only one of Trabzonspor’s last seven games saw both teams score.

Trabzonspor have failed to score in four of their last six European away fixtures.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Five of Ioannis Pittas’ last seven goals for CSKA Sofia have served as their first or last of the game, and they’ve never lost when he’s been on target (W21, D6). Trabzonspor’s Noah Saviolo has seen both of his goals this term arrive before the 35th minute and half-time, while he’s been fouled three times in each of his last two outings in this competition.

CSKA Sofia’s Léo Pereira is out injured, while Franculino remains an injury concern for Trabzonspor.
