FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

OFI Crete and Anderlecht enter matchday five of this season’s UEFA Europa League (UEL) side by side in the league-phase table, with both teams having won their last game in the competition. 

Match News and Current Form

OFI sit 16th ahead of this round of fixtures, only one place behind Anderlecht on goal difference, after beating Hapoel Be’er Sheva 2-0 on matchday four. That was their second clean-sheet win of the league phase (L2), which is perhaps surprising given that pre-round, Christos Kontis’ men have faced the most shots (95) and the second-most shots on target (33) in the UEL league phase. That approach has proven to be to their detriment at home, where OFI have won just one of their last four competitive games, but with five of their last six victories at Pankritio Stadium being accompanied by a clean sheet, defence is likely to be the bedrock of any potential success here.

Anderlecht have corrected the course of their UEL campaign with successive wins to nil across the last two rounds, and the Belgian outfit have the chance here to make it three consecutive European main-draw victories without conceding for the first time since November 2011. The most recent of those positive results came against Salzburg (2-0), with that win forming part of an ongoing four-match unbeaten run (W3, D1) for the Purple and White, which includes their 1-0 victory over Leuven on Sunday. Now looking towards emulating their best-ever performance in this competition from ten seasons ago when they reached the quarter-finals, Anderlecht may have to overcome some travelling demons as they’ve lost three of their last five competitive away games (W1, D1). 

Head-to-Head History

These sides have never played each other before, while OFI have never faced a Belgian team. Anderlecht have already gone up against a Greek outfit this season, beating PAOK 4-2 on aggregate in UEL qualifying. 

Hot Stats and Streaks

OFI have attempted the fewest shots (26) in this league phase.

Just one of OFI’s six European matches this term saw both teams score.

The team scoring first won each of Anderlecht’s last eight UEL games.

Pre-round, Anderlecht have suffered the third-most fouls in the UEL main draw this season (63).

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Taxiarchis Fountas scored OFI’s second against Be’er Sheva, the seventh successive goal of his to arrive before the hour mark. Marten Winkler was Anderlecht’s second goalscorer against Salzburg, meaning that he has now been on the winning side in his last 12 goalscoring appearances.

OFI’s Kenan Kodro and Krešimir Krizmanić missed their 2-0 loss to Panathinaikos at the weekend through injury, while Anderlecht don’t seem to have any fresh absentees.
