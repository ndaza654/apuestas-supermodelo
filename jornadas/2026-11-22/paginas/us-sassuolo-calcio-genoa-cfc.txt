FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

A Sassuolo side in freefall desperately need to end a miserable losing streak when they host Genoa, whose recent improvement has afforded them a five-point lead over their opponents here.

Match News and Current Form

No side in Serie A was more grateful for the timing of the international break than Sassuolo, who lost each of their last five matches. The Neroverdi haven’t endured a worse such streak in the top-flight since March 2014, and with their gap to the relegation zone down to a single point pre-round, they’re in desperate need of a turnaround. Going unbeaten in each of their last seven Serie A matches after an international break (W3, D4) would hint at one starting here, but Sassuolo have been incredibly porous at home, conceding ten times across their last two matches here!

Genoa may have been beaten 2-1 by AC Milan in their last outing, but three successive wins prior outline Daniele De Rossi’s side’s improvement. The Grifone last posted more points at this stage of a top-flight season back in 2018/19, but that will offer little encouragement given that they only avoided relegation on H2H record in that campaign. Genoa could go a long way to staying clear of another survival scrap with a win here, but supporters are unlikely to be too confident as that would require securing back-to-back Serie A victories on the road under the same manager for the first time since May 2021!

Head-to-Head History

Genoa have won each of the last four H2Hs, as many victories as they managed in the prior 17 Serie A meetings (D5, L8). They’ve avoided defeat on their last three trips to Sassuolo (W2, D1), but against no side do the Neroverdi boast more home wins in Serie A than Genoa (6).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Sassuolo’s Serie A matches this season produced a pre-round league-high 45 goals.

Sassuolo registered just four shots on target across their last four games.

Genoa won all four league matches they led at half-time this season.

Genoa failed to score in five of their last six Serie A away defeats.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Sassuolo forward Sebastiano Esposito could become just the third player in the last 65 years in Serie A to score against the same team for four different clubs before turning 25, having already netted a career-high three times against Genoa for three teams. Genoa’s Stephan El Shaarawy is chasing personal history here as he looks to assist in three successive starts for the first time in a top-five European league.

Sassuolo’s Vasilije Adžić suffered an injury on international duty, while Genoa’s Morten Frendrup is managing an ankle issue.

Betting Analysis

It’s hard to back the hosts at present, but as they’ve netted in each of the last 12 Serie A H2Hs, both teams to score looks a good option.
