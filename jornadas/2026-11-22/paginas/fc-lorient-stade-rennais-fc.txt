FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

After seeing their form nosedive in recent weeks, a trip to face a Lorient side who’ve had a hold over them in recent times hardly seems well timed for Rennes.

Match News and Current Form

Recent results have been trending in the right direction for Lorient, who have won three of their last five matches (D1, L1) to move within touching distance of the top half. Building upon last season’s ninth-place finish will be the ultimate goal this term, and their success has been largely built on a solid defence which has already kept six clean sheets this term, just one shy of their total across the entirety of last season. All four of their victories this season have been accompanied by a shutout, including both at home, which account for their only wins across their last seven home league games (D1, L4).

Rennes have slipped off the pace in the European race in recent weeks, losing three of their last four Ligue 1 outings to tumble down the table. There’s still room for optimism though, as the 16 points they’ve amassed after ten rounds are at least four more than they had at this stage in the three prior Ligue 1 seasons. Further progress looks a tall order for a Rennes side who have conceded an average of 2.38 goals per Ligue 1 away game in 2026 (31 in 13 matches), their highest in a calendar year since 1977 (2.7), and having leaked 4+ goals in each of their last three league road trips (L3), they’re unlikely to be relishing this fixture. 

Head-to-Head History

Lorient have won each of the last six Ligue 1 H2Hs, their longest-ever winning run against a single opponent in the top-flight. Rennes could now suffer seven Ligue 1 defeats in a row against the same opponent for the first time since 1962 and only the third time in history.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Lorient’s last five league games each produced under 2.5 goals. 

Lorient have scored a pre-round joint league-low one goal before the 30th minute this season.

Rennes’ away league matches this term averaged a comfortable league-high three first-half goals.

Only PSG have registered more shots on target than Rennes in Ligue 1 (61) pre-round.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Only one player in Ligue 1 has been fouled more often than Lorient’s Jean-Victor Makengo pre-round (28), with his two goals this season serving to close the scoring. Rennes should welcome back top scorer Estéban Lepaul here and he could make an early mark as he netted before half-time in all four scoring appearances this term.

Lorient’s Isaak Touré is ruled out, while Rennes’ Przemysław Frankowski picked up an injury on international duty with Poland.

Betting Analysis

Lorient’s impressive H2H record can’t be looked past, and when coupled with Rennes’ poor form, a home win at generous odds seems a solid play.
