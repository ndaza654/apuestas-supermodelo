FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Having rapidly climbed the Serie A table, Bologna will look to secure a fifth consecutive victory when they host a struggling Udinese side that spent the international break rooted to the bottom of the standings.

Match News and Current Form

Bologna’s appointment of Raffaele Palladino has so far paid off as they begin the round on Serie A’s longest ongoing winning streak, making it four straight victories with a 2-1 triumph at rivals Parma. Sitting just three points off the top six pre-round, the Rossoblù are firmly in the European qualification hunt after becoming only the fourth side in the division’s history to go without a victory in their opening five league games in a campaign before winning five of their next six. Bologna could now record five successive top-flight victories under a manager appointed during the season for the first time since 1956, but their home form needs improvement, as their 2-1 win over Monza late last month was their first at Stadio Renato Dall’Ara this term (D2, L2).

Udinese hit rock bottom before the international break, losing 2-1 at fellow strugglers Venezia in their first match under new boss Paolo Zanetti. They've picked up just five points so far this season (W1, D2, L8), and only once in their history have they lost more times in their opening 11 Serie A games in a campaign. They are consequently winless in nine games (D1, L8), the club’s longest top-flight drought since a 14-match run which ended in October 2023 (D7, L7), and the Little Zebras’ away form does not offer much encouragement either, as they have not won any of their last four such games (D1, L3).

Head-to-Head History

Bologna have lost just one of their last 13 meetings with Udinese (W4, D8), while seven of their last nine home H2Hs have seen both teams score.

Hot Stats and Streaks

A pre-round league-high seven of Bologna’s matches have been level at half-time this season (HT: W2, L2).

Bologna have scored 11 of their 16 league goals this term after the break.

Udinese have scored six of their eight goals away from home this season before half-time.

Four of Udinese’s five away matches this term have produced over 2.5 goals.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Bologna’s Samuel Mbangula has opened the scoring in five of his eight senior scoring appearances, even though six of his last nine goals have arrived after half-time. Udinese will be keen to see Keinan Davis get his first Serie A goal this season, as he has only ever been on the losing side once when scoring for them (W10, D4).

Bologna may be without Juan Miranda, while Nicolò Bertola is unavailable for Udinese.

Udinese’s away games are quite often high-scoring, so backing over 2.5 goals seems sensible here.
