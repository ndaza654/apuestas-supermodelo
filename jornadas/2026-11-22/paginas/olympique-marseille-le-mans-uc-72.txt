FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Marseille look to retain their status as Ligue 1’s quickest-improving side when they host newly-promoted Le Mans, who are struggling to adapt to life in the top flight. 

Match News and Current Form

Marseille will hope the international break hasn’t stunted their momentum, as a 2-1 win over Lens in their last outing was a fourth successive victory in all competitions and a fifth in a row in Ligue 1. The Olympians last posted a longer winning streak in the league back in January 2023, and repeating that feat would certainly help their European qualification bid as they start the round just two points adrift of the top three. An outcome either way looks likely as Marseille are the only side yet to draw a Ligue 1 game this season, a trend they’re heavily favoured to continue as they bid to win three competitive home games in succession for the first time in over a year here.

The step up to Ligue 1 has been a tough one for Le Mans, who are without a win in five (D2, L3) and start the round outside the bottom three on goal difference alone. Drawing more games (5) than any other side in the division across the first ten rounds has helped compensate for posting just one win - their second-lowest number of victories at this stage of a top-flight season. They’ll need to upset the odds to add to that tally here, and another defeat would mark their first set of successive away losses in the same league campaign since March 2023. 

Head-to-Head History

Le Mans have never won away at Marseille in the top-flight (D2, L4), and have posted just two wins across 12 Ligue 1 H2Hs at any venue (D5, L5).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Marseille have failed to score in just one of their last 26 home league matches.

Marseille have conceded a pre-round league-high seven headed goals this season.

Le Mans have won just one of the four Ligue 1 games they scored first in this term (D3).

Le Mans start the round as one of three sides without a clean sheet in Ligue 1 this season.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Having scored in each of Marseille’s last three games, Amine Gouiri is set to face a 29th different opponent in Ligue 1 - he’s netted against 25 of the prior 28. Erwan Colas marked his return from injury with a 90th-minute equaliser against Monaco last time out, but each of his prior four strikes for Le Mans came before half-time.

Emerson has missed Marseille’s last two games, just as Djibril Sidibé has for Le Mans.
