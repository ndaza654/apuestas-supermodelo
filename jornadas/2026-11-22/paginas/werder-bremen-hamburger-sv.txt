FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

The 157th competitive Nordderby closes out the weekend action, with Werder Bremen and Hamburg separated by just three points in the Bundesliga table.

Match News and Current Form

Sitting pretty in sixth place heading into the international break, Werder Bremen will likely have to get back to winning ways to retain that spot after their seven-game Bundesliga unbeaten run ended in the last round with a 1-0 loss at Stuttgart. Ten points from just four home league games so far represents Bremen’s best start in 15 years since achieving 12 at this stage in 2011/12, with that run affording them the chance to register a first run of five unbeaten home league games to open a season since 2005/06. Yet to drop a point when taking the lead this term (W4), Bremen will hope a quick start against their great rivals can prove decisive. 

Hamburg actually match that record perfectly with a four-game winning return when taking the lead, with the most recent of those coming in their latest game (3-1 v Gladbach) to move them within three points of their great rivals. Led by Hamburg-born coach Merlin Polzin, the visitors will be under no illusions about the significance of this game as they continue their upwards trajectory which sees them with four more points than at the same stage of their Bundesliga return last season. Despite the positives, Hamburg’s woeful travelling record (W1, L4) is a major cause for concern, with only rock-bottom Gladbach losing so often on the road. 

Head-to-Head History

This will be the 111th Bundesliga H2H, with Bremen slightly edging this fixture with 40 wins to Hamburg’s 35 (D35). At home though, Bremen have picked up more points against Hamburg (W29, D16, L10) than any team except Cologne. Bremen has also faced Hamburg at least 25 more times in all competitions than any other opponent in their club history.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Bremen have scored 14 of their 17 league goals after half-time. 

Bremen have avoided defeat in four of their six league games when conceding first (W1, D3, L2). 

Hamburg’s nine league games were all won by the team that scored first (W4, L5). 

Hamburg have conceded as many home league goals after the 75th minute (G3) as they did in the entire first half. 

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Niclas Füllkrug is the first Werder Bremen player to score in four consecutive Bundesliga home games since Max Kruse in 2017, although he is yet to score against Hamburg in three H2Hs. Hamburg’s Miro Muheim has scored three goals from outside of the box, a tally bettered only by Bayern’s Michael Olise. Two of those three strikes were also decisive in victory. 

Mitchell Weiser is an injury doubt for Bremen, while Hamburg’s Terem Moffi and Fábio Vieira are also doubtful.
