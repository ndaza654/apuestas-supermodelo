FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Newly promoted Racing Santander and fifth-placed Real Sociedad will be looking to build some positive momentum in this Sunday clash after they both bounced back with victories prior to the international break.

Match News and Current Form

Racing’s 2-1 triumph over Málaga propelled them into La Liga’s top ten ahead of this round of fixtures and was their second win from their last three top-flight games (L1), as many wins as they had achieved in their previous 29 such matches. Their latest victory was yet another example of them coming from behind, with José Alberto’s men having earned more points from losing positions than anyone else in the division (ten) after the end of last round. As expected, the Cantabrians have built much of their success this term at home, picking up 11 of their 14 points at El Sardinero. 

Real Sociedad’s 1-0 win over Rayo Vallecano earlier this month saw them strengthen their grip on fifth spot and means that, between the two most recent international breaks, only Real Madrid (15) had earned more La Liga points than their 12. The Basque side should not be bothered by the pressure of playing away from home, having won three of their last four such league games (L1) - as many victories as in their previous 25 top-flight matches on their travels. But should Pellegrino Matarazzo’s men fall behind here, it appears unlikely that they will come back into the contest, having not gained a single point from a losing position this season. 

Head-to-Head History

Racing face the prospect of completing three consecutive top-flight H2Hs without scoring for the first time (currently D1, L1).

Hot Stats and Streaks

At least one side has scored 2+ goals in Racing’s last 11 games. 

Racing’s last seven La Liga goals were all scored after half-time.

Real Sociedad’s last eight league games each saw the team that scored first go on to win.

None of Real Sociedad’s last 12 La Liga goals came after the 75th minute. 

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Yassir Zabiri continues to impress for Racing, with his winner against Málaga seeing him record his seventh successive scoring appearance for club or country at senior level in which both teams have found the net (W5, D1, L1). Real Sociedad’s Orri Óskarsson made the difference against Rayo, and after a successful international break with Iceland, he has now registered 12 goal involvements (G9, A3) in his last 12 games. 

Racing seemingly have no fresh absentees. Chelsea loanee Mamadou Sarr missed Real Sociedad’s win over Rayo because of a calf problem, while teammate Ander Barrenetxea had to be withdrawn during that game and is set to be a long-term casualty.
