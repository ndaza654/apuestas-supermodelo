FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Osasuna must fancy their chances of creating some breathing space between themselves and the La Liga relegation zone as they host a Málaga side yet to win in the league this term.

Match News and Current Form

Since going unbeaten in their opening three La Liga matches this season (W2, D1), things have unravelled for Osasuna, who start matchday 13 just two points above the drop zone. A run of just one victory in nine attempts in the league (D2, L6) is the result of some poor defending as they enter this clash having conceded 23 goals this term - only Racing Santander conceded more across the first 12 rounds. Osasuna should fancy their chances here though as just four of those goals came at El Sadar where they’ve also picked up nine of their 12 points this season (W2, D3, L1).

With a 2-1 defeat to Racing Santander last time out despite scoring first, Málaga became just the 13th side in Spanish top-flight history to fail to win any of their opening 12 matches of a campaign (D5, L7). Given that 11 of the prior 12 were eventually relegated, the visitors’ return to the top flight looks set to be short-lived. Málaga are without a win in 17 La Liga away games (D4, L13) stretching back to December 2017, and a measly return of three goals from their six such trips this season (D2, L4) hardly bodes well here. 

Head-to-Head History

Osasuna have won just 7.7% of their La Liga home H2Hs against Málaga (W1, D7, L5), their lowest win percentage against an opponent they’ve faced 5+ times at home in competition history.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Osasuna’s home La Liga matches this season have produced a league-low average of 1.5 goals per game.

Only two teams start the round having picked up more yellow cards than Osasuna in La Liga this term (33).

Eight of Málaga’s 12 La Liga matches this season saw both teams score.

Málaga have faced a pre-round league-high 197 shots this campaign.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Osasuna's Ante Budimir has eight goals in 12 La Liga appearances this season, with three of his last four opening the scoring. Carlos Dotor netted Malaga’s last goal, with both of his strikes this season serving as match openers before the half-hour mark.

Osasuna are without a number of players including Abel Bretones, while Málaga are without the suspended Adam Aznou.

Despite their recent woes, Osasuna usually play better on home soil so Osasuna to win without conceding could be a great option.
