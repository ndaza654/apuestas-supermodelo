FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Liverpool’s search for consistency continues as they look to take advantage of their fierce yet wounded rivals Manchester United, who begin life under new management yet again.

Match News and Current Form

Liverpool went into the international break on a winning note after beating Crystal Palace 3-1 away from home, a well needed result considering they lost their two matches prior. The Reds start the weekend in fourth place having won half of their league games this term (D3, L2), but surprisingly it’s been at Anfield where they’ve struggled having secured just five of their 18 points at home, the lowest ratio of any side in 2026/27. Their last home league game saw them lose 1-0 to Arsenal, meaning Liverpool are at risk of losing back-to-back home top-flight matches for the first time since March 2021.

Despite twice coming from behind to draw 4-4 with Aston Villa in their last game, Michael Carrick’s time as Manchester United manager came to an end prematurely after a historically bad start to the campaign that’s landed them in the relegation zone. Eight points after ten games is their lowest haul since 1986/87, a tally made up of just one win and no clean sheets. The man tasked with turning things around is former Tottenham and Chelsea manager Mauricio Pochettino - who becomes United’s first boss to start his league tenure against Liverpool - which looks far from ideal given that he’s won just three of his 19 meetings with them in the past (D5, L11).

Head-to-Head History

Liverpool have won just one of the last seven H2Hs (D3, L3) after losing both league games last term, which was as many wins as United had managed over them in the previous 18 league meetings (D9, L7). The Red Devils have avoided defeat in three successive visits to Anfield (W1, D2).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Liverpool have won the corner count in each of their last eight games.

Only one side trailed more Premier League games at half-time across the first ten rounds than Liverpool (4).

United are one of two teams pre-round yet to win a Premier League game when scoring first (D3).

United have only scored one first-half goal away from home this league season.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Liverpool’s Cody Gakpo has scored in three successive games against United and has netted a career-high five H2H goals against the Red Devils in the Premier League, four of which came at Anfield. United’s Bryan Mbeumo scored the opener in this fixture last term, and he’ll be looking to replicate the feat having netted the game’s first goal in his last three scoring outings for his club.

Liverpool look to have no fresh issues, while United will hope Luke Shaw and Marcus Rashford can return here.

Betting Analysis

Liverpool will be wary of the potential new manager bounce effect and may start cautiously, meaning a half-time draw could well be on the cards.
