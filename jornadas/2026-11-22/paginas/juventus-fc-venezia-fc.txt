FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Juventus look to close in on Serie A’s top six when they take on newly-promoted Venezia, who have finally climbed off the bottom of the Serie A table.

Match News and Current Form

Juventus started to trend upwards before the international break, losing just one of their last six matches in all competitions (W3, D2) after twice coming from behind to draw 2-2 at Fiorentina. Pressure has therefore started to lift off Luciano Spalletti, who becomes only the sixth manager to take charge of 600 Serie A matches here and will be confident of marking the occasion in style given that the Bianconeri have collected 22 of a possible 24 points across their last eight league games against newly-promoted clubs. Scoring 21 times overall while conceding just once in the last six matches of that sequence highlights Juventus’ dominance in such games, and losing just once at home this term (W4, D2) further underlines their favourites tag here.

Venezia recorded a valuable 2-1 home victory over fellow strugglers Udinese before the international break, snapping a three-match losing streak to climb off the bottom of the table. Just two wins from their opening 11 rounds and a league-low eight goals scored suggests that survival specialist manager Davide Nicola still faces a tough task, with the Winged Lions last winning back-to-back Serie A games in November 2021. Claiming three points on the road won't be easy either, as Venezia have lost their last six top-flight away matches, and could suffer seven successive Serie A road defeats for the first time since 2001 here.

Head-to-Head History

Juventus have won seven of their last nine outings against Venezia (D2), despite conceding in each of the last five. The Bianconeri have scored 2+ goals in six of their last seven home league H2Hs.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Juventus are yet to score in the opening 15 minutes of either half in Serie A this season.

Exactly three Juventus players were booked in each of their last three matches in all competitions.

Venezia are the only Serie A side yet to lead at half-time this term (HT: D6, L5).

Venezia have registered 4+ shots on target in six of their last seven games.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Gleison Bremer has won 11 of his 14 scoring appearances for Juventus (D3), with four of his last six home goals being match openers. Venezia’s Toma Bašić has scored three of his four Serie A goals after the break, with the only exception arriving against Juventus last season.

Pierre Kalulu and Zeki Çelik were added to Juventus’ long injury list which still includes Kenan Yıldız, while Venezia’s Pasquale Mazzocchi was taken off injured in their last game.

Betting Analysis

Both sides have done the bulk of their scoring after the interval while struggling to make an impact early on, making the second half to be the highest-scoring half a strong angle.
