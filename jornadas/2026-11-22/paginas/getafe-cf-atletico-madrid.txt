FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Getafe’s form at home has been the sole reason they’ve been able to stay out of the bottom three but that record will be tested by Atlético Madrid, as boss Diego Simeone pursues a historic victory.

Match News and Current Form

The international break appeared to come at an appropriate time for Getafe after manager José Bordalás blamed fatigue for a fadeout at Villarreal in which they conceded twice after the 75th minute to lose 2-0. Few clubs in Europe’s top-five leagues have endured such an enormous disparity between their home and away form quite like Getafe, who are outside of the relegation zone only by way of collecting all 12 of their points at the Estadio Coliseum (W3, D3), where they are now on their longest unbeaten run in this competition (W5, D3) since a run of 11 games in 2023 (W7, D4).

Atlético Madrid were left to wonder what might have been after Giuliano Simeone’s tenth-minute opener was cancelled out on the way to a 2-1 loss at home to Barcelona that saw them spend the international break five points from the summit, instead of at its peak. Diego Simeone would not criticise his players for denying him a record-breaking 345th La Liga victory, particularly as the club are still enjoying their equal-best start to a league campaign since they last won the title in 2020/21. Now the focus is on registering a fifth consecutive away win in this competition for the third time in Simeone’s long tenure, having previously done so in 2021 and 2014. 

Head-to-Head History

Getafe collected points from three of the last seven La Liga H2Hs (W1, D2, L4), but the success has been modest and short-lived. Diego Simeone could equal Luis Aragonés’ La Liga record for most wins against one opposition with what would be a 23rd win over Getafe here (D4, L1).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Getafe have lost five of their seven La Liga matches as a betting outsider this season (W1, D1). 

Only four of Getafe’s last 20 La Liga home games produced over 2.5 total goals.

Atlético have opened the scoring in a league-high 11 of their first 12 La Liga games.

Six of Atlético’s last seven competitive games saw under 2.5 total goals scored.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Enes Ünal scored in Getafe’s last La Liga home game and has thrived in this matchup, with his five goals against Atlético his most against any one opponent in this competition. Atlético’s Lee Kang-in has played more competitive games against Getafe (ten) than any other club, scoring a brace for Mallorca the last time he met them.

Getafe’s Zaid Romero is questionable after only recently returning to training, whilst Atlético have doubts over Alejandro Grimaldo, Cristian Romero and Marc Pubill.
