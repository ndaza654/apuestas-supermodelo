FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Barcelona look to maintain their cushion at the La Liga summit as they welcome resurgent top-six chasers Villarreal to Camp Nou.

Match News and Current Form

A near-perfect record of 33 points from a possible 36 across the opening 12 La Liga rounds has seen Barcelona fly to their joint-fourth strongest start to a season. Despite that rampant record, the Blaugrana entered the international break with only a three-point cushion at the top of the table, so securing a victory is a necessity for the hosts to at least maintain that gap. Barcelona’s lethal attack will be called upon again as they’ve netted four goals per game on average in La Liga this term, a figure which actually increases across their last five home league outings (W4, L1), in which Hansi Flick’s men have netted 22 times! 

Making the trip to Barcelona from a town whose entire population couldn't fill the Camp Nou, Villarreal look to cause an upset as they once again look to establish themselves as European contenders. The Yellow Submarine are a steadily improving outfit under Iñigo Pérez, whose side have picked up seven points across the last four rounds (W2, D1, L1), only one fewer than they had managed across the opening eight. Villarreal are unlikely to continue trending in the right direction here though, as their only two away league wins this term came against the current bottom two, while they failed to beat any of the top four both home and away (D1, L2). 

Head-to-Head History

Barcelona have lost two of the last five La Liga H2Hs (W3), which is twice as many as they suffered in the 29 clashes prior (W22, D6, L1). No team has posted more La Liga away wins against Barcelona since the start of the 2021/22 season than Villarreal (3).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Barcelona have picked up a pre-round joint league-low 18 yellow cards.

Barcelona have won three of the four league games they conceded first in this season (L1). 

Villarreal have led a joint league-low one game at half-time this term (HT: D8, L3).

Villarreal are yet to score a goal before the 30-minute mark in away league action. 

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Barcelona’s Fermín López has been involved in 12 goals in 11 La Liga appearances this season (G7, A5) - no midfielder has contributed to more goals in their first 11 games of a La Liga season in this century. After assisting from the bench in Villarreal’s last game, Nicolas Pépé will be chasing a start here given that five of his last six La Liga goals came before half-time.

Pau Cubarsí is a doubt for Barcelona after picking up an injury with Spain, while Villarreal have doubts over Gerard Moreno, Ilias Akhomach and Juan Foyth.
