FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Atalanta’s poor recent H2H record gives Inter Milan hope of reaching ten wins in their first 12 Serie A matches of a campaign for just the fifth time in history.

Match News and Current Form

Atalanta earned a much-needed 1-0 win over Monza before the international break, bringing a timely end to their six-game winless streak (D3, L3) in all competitions. Maurizio Sarri’s men may have their work cut out to follow that up at the New Balance Arena as the hosts have failed to win their last three competitive games in Bergamo (D2, L1). Four wins in five games prior at this venue (L1) underline their strengths in familiar conditions, and any success here may come in a close-fought contest as five of Atalanta’s last six Serie A home wins came by a single-goal margin.

Inter scored twice within the first 20 minutes to win 2-0 against Como and go into the international break one point behind leaders Lazio. The Nerazzurri are in fine form at present on the back of six wins from their last seven competitive matches (L1), while they haven’t yet been beaten away from the San Siro in the Italian top-flight this season (W3, D1). Some of that success has been built on finishing games strongly, as Inter have gained a league-high nine points from goals scored after the 75th minute, while a win here would see them secure a fourth consecutive victory away from the San Siro.

Head-to-Head History

Atalanta are winless in the last 15 Serie A H2Hs (D6, L9), with seven of those defeats coming in the last eight matches (D1) within that run.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Only two sides in Serie A have committed fewer fouls than Atalanta’s 106 pre-round.

The last nine goals in Atalanta home matches have arrived after the 40th minute.

Pre-round, Inter have had the most shots (234) and faced the fewest (108) in Serie A this season.

The opening goal arrived within the first 15 minutes in eight of Inter’s 11 league games this term.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Nikola Krstović racked up five goal contributions for Montenegro during the international break (G3, A2), while Atalanta have gained seven points in Serie A this season from his goals. Krstović scored Atalanta’s goal when these sides last met, and Francesco Pio Esposito netted for Inter that day - he’s scored before half-time in four successive matches for club or country.

Atalanta’s Gianluca Gaetano remains sidelined, while Giacomo Raspadori will be assessed close to kick-off. Inter’s Ange-Yoan Bonny is injured, while Aleksandar Stanković withdrew from the Serbian national camp in the break with an injury.

Atalanta’s inconsistency at home coupled with Inter’s recent H2H record makes an away win a distinct possibility in this fixture.
