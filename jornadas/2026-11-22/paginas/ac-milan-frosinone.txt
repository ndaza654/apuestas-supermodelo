FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

AC Milan welcome Serie A surprise package Frosinone to the San Siro, with the promoted side arriving just four points behind their hosts.

Match News and Current Form

Milan have lost just once in Serie A this term (W7, D3), establishing themselves as potential title contenders under Ruben Amorim, albeit they’re already playing catch-up on that front. Their gap to the summit would’ve been even wider had they not beaten Inter Milan 3-1 in their last home league outing, a result which continued their impressive pattern of scoring at least twice in all five of their home league games this season (W4, L1). Continuing that impressive run here could be easier said than done here, as the Rossoneri have only won three of their last seven league fixtures immediately after an international break (D2, L2).

Frosinone have made light work of the step up from Serie B (W6, D2, L3), and they could become just the fourth promoted side in the last 40 years to win seven of their opening 12 Serie A fixtures of the season. Massimiliano Alvini’s attack-minded side have earned their shot at history by outscoring even Milan (24-23), although their momentum was halted with a 3-1 defeat to Cagliari on matchday 11 which ended a four-match unbeaten run (W3, D1). Bouncing back on the road appears a tall order for the Canaries, who are winless in four away league games (D2, L2) since beating Fiorentina 3-0 on matchday two. 

Head-to-Head History

Milan are unbeaten across all six previous H2Hs (W4, D2), scoring at least twice in each of their three home meetings (W2, D1).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Milan have conceded nine of their 13 Serie A goals this season in the second half.

Milan have opened the scoring in four of their five home league games this term.

Frosinone’s Serie A matches have averaged a pre-round league-high 2.09 first-half goals.

Frosinone’s away league fixtures this season averaged 14.6 corners per game.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Adrien Rabiot assisted against Genoa, becoming the first Milan midfielder since Kaká in 2004 to produce at least one goal contribution in six consecutive Serie A appearances, while four of his five league goals this term fired Milan into a 2-0 lead. Farès Ghedjemis has five goal contributions across his last four league appearances (G1, A4), with each of his last six club goals arriving after half-time.

Milan could be without as many as seven players here, while Frosinone’s Antonio Raimondo is injured and Gabriele Bracaglia is suspended.
