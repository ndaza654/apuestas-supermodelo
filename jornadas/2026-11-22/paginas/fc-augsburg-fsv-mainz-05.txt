FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Augsburg and Mainz will both be desperate to secure a win this weekend after entering the international break in very poor form and conceding far too many goals.

Match News and Current Form

Despite scoring after only 37 seconds away to RB Leipzig in their last game, Augsburg found themselves on the receiving end of a 5-2 thumping that extended their winless league run to seven fixtures (D3, L4). This is their longest streak without a victory in the Bundesliga since late 2022/23, when they also went seven games without a win, and if Manuel Baum’s side fail to pick up three points on Sunday, they could find themselves in the bottom three by the end of the round. Whilst scoring goals has not proved to be an issue for Augsburg, who have set a new club record by scoring in each of their first nine games of a new Bundesliga campaign, they have not won a home match since August (D3, L1). 

Mainz supporters will be hoping that their team has used the international window as a circuit-breaker after they lost four successive games across all competitions, including three in the Bundesliga. It’s their longest losing streak in this competition since recording four in a row between September and October 2025, and they will drop below their opponents if they are beaten again this weekend. They too have been regular goalscorers like their hosts, striking in each of their last eight Bundesliga fixtures and each of their last ten on the road, which has contributed to them collecting all three of their league wins this season on the road and sitting behind only Bayern Munich for Bundesliga away points after nine rounds.

Head-to-Head History

Mainz have not lost any of their last five Bundesliga games against Augsburg (W4, D1) and have won on three of their last four visits (L1) to the WWK Arena.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Augsburg have been ahead at some stage in eight of their nine league games this season (W2, D3, L4).

Augsburg committed the fewest fouls in the first nine rounds of the Bundesliga this season (83).

Only Borussia Mönchengladbach had committed more fouls than Mainz (120) in the first nine rounds of this term.

Mainz have won eight of their last 11 Bundesliga away games as a betting favourite (L3). 

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Fabian Rieder has contributed to a goal in seven of Augsburg’s nine games (G2, A5) with five of those contributions coming after half-time. Phillip Tietz got his sixth league goal of this campaign in round nine and has already scored Mainz’s opener five times across all competitions this season.

Augsburg will need to make late calls over Jeffrey Gouweleeuw and Han-Noah Massengo while Mainz have no new availability issues.

With both teams struggling for wins but still scoring goals, backing both teams to score looks like a smart approach.
