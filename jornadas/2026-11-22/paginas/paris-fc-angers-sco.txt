FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Flying high among the early title pacesetters, Paris FC are fighting firmly at the opposite end of the table to an Angers side in the relegation mix-up at this early stage.

Match News and Current Form

Paris FC have made light work of their second season in Ligue 1, posting six victories across their first ten games (D2, L2). The capital club are already over halfway to matching their win tally from last season (11), and their impressive start is perhaps best illustrated by the fact their four previous top-flight campaigns saw them have to wait until at least matchday 18 to pick up their sixth win! Solid home form has been the foundation of their success, with four victories from their five home league games so far this term (D1), part of a wider run of nine wins in their last 11 Ligue 1 outings at Stade Jean-Bouin (D1, L1).

Angers are sitting firmly at the wrong end of the table on the back of a five-match winless run (D1, L4), with both of their victories this season coming prior to the September international break. It’s been something of a long-standing demise for Angers, who start the round in the bottom three and have now lost 16 Ligue 1 matches in 2026 (W5, D7), more than any other side, with six of the last seven of those defeats to come on the road alarmingly seeing them lose by a 2+ goal margin. 

Head-to-Head History

Paris have won just one of the last five H2Hs (D2, L2), while Angers were the only side they failed to score against in both Ligue 1 H2Hs last season.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Paris are the only side yet to trail a Ligue 1 game at half-time this season (HT: W6, D4).

Only one side has collected more yellow cards than Paris (22) in Ligue 1 this term pre-round.

Just three sides committed fewer fouls across the first ten rounds than Angers (105).

Angers conceded six of their eight away league goals this campaign in the first half. 

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Paris’ Hamari Traoré is in line to make his 250th Ligue 1 appearance here and he’s won a career-high ten matches against Angers, and has only picked up more yellow cards against one side than his five in this fixture. Angers’ Branco van den Boomen is becoming well-known to the referee having committed 2+ fouls in four of his last five Ligue 1 appearances. 

Adama Camara has missed Paris’ last two matches, while Angers’ Ousmane Camara was stretchered off on international duty for Mali.

Betting Analysis

Everything points towards this game being rather one-sided, so backing Paris to win by a -1 handicap could be a good route to value.
