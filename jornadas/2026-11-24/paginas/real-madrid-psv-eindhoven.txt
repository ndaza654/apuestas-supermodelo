FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Real Madrid seek a fifth successive UEFA Champions League (UCL) victory this season as they host a PSV side struggling to make an impact on Europe despite their domestic dominance.

Match News and Current Form

Real Madrid left it late to come from behind and beat Celta Vigo 2-1 in La Liga on Saturday, extending their winning run in all competitions to nine matches. That includes three of their four UCL victories, putting Los Blancos in a strong position to directly qualify for the last 16, although conceding seven times in those games is a concern for José Mourinho’s side. The 15-time European champions are making the Bernabéu a fortress again despite that defensive vulnerability, winning all eight competitive games here this term, including UCL victories over Inter Milan (2-1) and Leipzig (4-1).

Eredivisie leaders PSV have scored 16 goals across their last three league games (W3), but have been unable to replicate that sparkling attacking form in Europe this term (W1, D1, L2). Peter Bosz’s side have scored just three times in the UCL and start the matchday just inside the top 24 after losing 2-1 to Club Brugge on matchday four. That was a fifth defeat in their last seven UCL matches (W1, D1), though the sole victory came on the road at Porto in their last European trip and makes up part of a respectable run of form across their last seven away UCL fixtures (W2, D3, L2).

Head-to-Head History

This is a first H2H since the 1988/89 European Cup quarter-finals, with Madrid losing only one of the eight previous meetings (W3, D4) and remaining unbeaten at home (W3, D1).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Madrid landed exactly eight shots on target in three of their four UCL fixtures this term.

Four of Madrid’s last five UCL home games produced exactly three goals.

PSV are yet to score a UCL goal before half-time this season.

PSV conceded exactly once in four of their last six away UCL fixtures.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Kylian Mbappé has scored four goals in as many UCL appearances this season, with three of his last four goals in any competition arriving between the 70th and 90th minute. Guus Til, who scored PSV’s winner at Porto, has five goal contributions across his last seven appearances (G3, A2) and four of his last six goals came between the 20th and 30th minute.

Madrid have no new injury concerns, while PSV’s Mauro Júnior could be a doubt after being substituted during the first half of PSV’s weekend win over NEC.
