FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Desperate to keep their UEFA Champions League (UCL) knockout hopes alive, RB Leipzig welcome a Lens side enduring a turbulent spell and still reeling from a historic humiliation in the last round.

Match News and Current Form

RB Leipzig are enduring a difficult European campaign, with a 3-2 loss to Manchester City marking their third defeat in four UCL (W1). They’re not experiencing the same issues domestically as following Saturday’s 5-1 thrashing of Union Berlin, they’ve now won four successive Bundesliga matches, form they’ll be desperate to transfer into this competition. That could prove difficult for a Leipzig side who’ve lost ten of their last 12 UCL matches (W2), but having won six of their seven home games this term (L1), the German side would be forgiven for expecting better fortunes here.

Lens boast an identical record to Leipzig after four UCL rounds, as after opening their campaign with a 3-2 victory at Slavia Prague, they have suffered three consecutive defeats. That slump culminated in an unprecedented 9-1 home humiliation against Como on matchday four, with that the heaviest home defeat any side has ever suffered in the UCL proper. Hitting the road is unlikely to offer much respite for a side who have won just one of their last five competitive away games (D1, L3), losing the last two without scoring, and a lack of goals explains why they’ve led for a competition-low 1.2% of their UCL matches this season.

Head-to-Head History

RB Leipzig and Lens square off for the very first time, though the hosts’ recent record against French clubs makes for grim reading having won only one of their last eight such matches (D2, L5).

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

With his goal at the weekend, RB Leipzig defender Ridle Baku has already doubled his scoring tally from last season, with six of his last seven strikes for club or country coming after the break. Returning from injury, Abdallah Sima netted on matchday one of the UCL, and has scored his last 11 goals at club level on the road.

RB Leipzig’s Assan Ouedraogo is out until the new year. Lens hope to have Franjo Ivanović back after he missed Saturday’s game, while Ruben Aguilar’s ongoing hamstring issue suggests his minutes could be managed.
