FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Galatasaray look to continue their resurgent form in the UEFA Champions League (UCL) and cut the gap to an out-of-form sixth-placed Aston Villa. 

Match News and Current Form

After maintaining their cushion at the peak of the Turkish Süper Lig with a sixth straight victory at the weekend, Galatasaray now return to European action in buoyant mood. After winning their last two UCL games by two-goal margins, Galatasaray can now equal their best-ever run of European Cup/UCL victories with a third in succession here, and that’s arguably a realistic aim, given the only side to beat them as the designated home side in all competitions this season are La Liga behemoths Barcelona (W7, D1, L1). 

Aston Villa make this trip on the back of a poor run of form which has seen them follow up six wins from seven competitive games with a five-game winless run (D3, L2). That prior good work includes Villa’s widest margin of victory in a European competition achieved against another Turkish side (6-0 vs Fenerbahçe) and has contributed to their strong UCL start (W3, L1). Victory is likely a necessity for Villa to remain amongst the top-eight sides in the group, as Unai Emery aims to avoid consecutive European group/league phase losses for the first time after 26 such games in charge. 

Head-to-Head History

This is the first-ever H2H meeting, but English sides have failed to win on nine of their last ten visits to face Galatasaray (D3, L6). Villa have lost just once across eight games against Turkish sides in Europe though (W6, D1).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Galatasaray have lost just one of their last 25 UCL games when leading at half-time (W15, D9). 

Galatasaray have scored first in all four of their UCL league phase games. 

Villa’s last ten UCL matches produced an average of 4.1 goals per game. 

The side scoring first has won all four of Villa’s UCL league phase games. 

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Aleksey Batrakov leads the way for Galatasaray with four UCL goals, three of which arrived at home. Overall he has an xG of just 1.06, marking him down as the biggest overperformer in the UCL this season (+2.94). With a UCL goal or assist every 78 minutes on average since the start of last season, Villa’s Nicolas Jackson has a better ratio than all but Harry Kane, Kylian Mbappé and Ousmane Dembélé amongst players to feature for 500+ minutes in the same period. 

Victor Osimhen headlines Galatasaray’s injury list, while Aston Villa’s Matty Cash will miss out after picking up an injury with Poland.
