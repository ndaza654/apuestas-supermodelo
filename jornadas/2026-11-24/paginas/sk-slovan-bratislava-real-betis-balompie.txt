FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Twelve years on from lifting the Premier League together at Manchester City, Yaya Touré and Manuel Pellegrini lock horns on the touchline in the UEFA Champions League (UCL) as Slovan Bratislava host Real Betis.

Match News and Current Form

Slovan were the only side to lose all of their first four league phase games this term, and sit at the bottom of the table as a result. All four of those defeats have come by a 3+ goal margin, with their 17 goals conceded the second-most by any side after the opening four games of a UCL campaign. Touré was appointed head coach in summer after Slovan had lifted their eighth consecutive Slovakian title, and though the Sky Blues are now unbeaten in ten domestic games (W6, D4), they have lost all 12 of their matches in the UCL proper - just one short of the competition’s all-time losing streak.

Real Betis are competing in the UCL for only the second time, but have taken the challenge in their stride. In his seventh season at the helm, Pellegrini’s side boasted a 100% league phase record prior to their 2-1 loss at Dortmund on matchday four, so it’s no surprise the Spaniards are overwhelming favourites here. That defeat is their only loss in their last seven competitive matches (W4, D2), and given that Pellegrini has never lost back-to-back group/league phase games in this competition, they’ve every reason to be confident of closing in on the top eight here. 

Head-to-Head History

These sides have never met previously. Slovan have won just one of their nine H2Hs against Spanish clubs (D2, L6), while Betis are facing a Slovakian side for the first time.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Only three of Slovan’s last nine games saw both teams score.

Betis registered over 14.5 shots in seven of their last ten competitive matches.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Rahim Ibrahim awaits his first goal involvement in the UCL proper, but has registered four in his last seven competitive appearances for Slovan (G3, A1). Betis’ Rodrigo Riquelme is also yet to score in the UCL but he’s netted the match opener in three of his four scoring appearances this term, with his last four strikes all coming on the road.

Slovan appear likely to remain without Roman Čerepkai and Suleiman Camara. Betis may be without Antony after he was forced off against rivals Sevilla, with Dani Ceballos also a major doubt.

Betting Analysis

Given how one-sided this game is widely expected to be and how badly Slovan have fared in Europe this term, Betis to win both halves looks very viable.
