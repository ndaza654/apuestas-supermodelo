FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

High-flying Como look to tighten their grip on a UEFA Champions League (UCL) top-eight berth when they host AEK Athens, who arrive undefeated across five previous meetings against Italian opposition.

Match News and Current Form

Sitting on nine points after four UCL rounds, Como enter the matchday as Italy’s top-performing side in the competition following their historic 9-1 demolition of Lens. That result put them firmly in contention for a top-eight finish, with their unstoppable European attack already netting 19 goals, just one shy of joining only three other clubs in UCL/European Cup history since 1955 to reach 20+ goals across their first-ever five matches! They also warmed up well with a 2-0 home league win over Cagliari at the weekend, making that seven victories from their last eight outings (L1) and maintaining an unbeaten home record this term (W6, D1).

AEK Athens opened their UCL campaign with a victory, yet they have suffered three consecutive defeats since - conceding more goals than every side except Lens over that span to complicate their knockout-stage hopes. An immediate European turnaround looks unlikely, as AEK are winless across all 17 of their away UCL matches (D5, L12), an all-time competition record since 1992/93, having already conceded eight goals in two road games this term. Still, the Greek side have lifted spirits with back-to-back domestic victories, including a 1-0 away win on Saturday, snapping four consecutive competitive defeats on their travels.

Head-to-Head History

This will be the first competitive meeting between these two sides, although AEK Athens are unbeaten in their last five competitive matches against Italian opposition (W2, D3).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Eight of Como’s last ten victories came by at least a two-goal margin.

AEK Athens have conceded 2+ goals in four of their last five away fixtures.

Nine of AEK’s last 11 matches produced over 2.5 goals.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Fresh off a hat-trick against Lens, Anastasios Douvikas has ended on the winning side in 21 of the last 23 matches in which he has scored for Como. For AEK, Stavro Pilo has struck after the break in two of their UCL defeats this campaign, netting seven of his previous eight goals in the second half.

Como came through their weekend league outing unscathed with no new injury concerns, whereas AEK Athens suffered a blow as Kervin Arriaga was forced off during their domestic weekend fixture, whilst Aboubakary Koita will be suspended after his sending off against Real Madrid.
