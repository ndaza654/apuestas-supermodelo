FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

In the midst of a domestic wobble, Manchester City turn their attentions to the UEFA Champions League (UCL) where they bid to down a Napoli side whose winless away run in this competition is close to reaching record territory.

Match News and Current Form

Only a favourable result elsewhere prevented City from losing further ground in the Premier League title race over the weekend, as they laboured to a 1-1 draw against Fulham. Enzo Maresca’s men haven’t won inside 90 minutes in their last five domestic matches (D2, L3), but they’ve fared better in the UCL, where they’ve won three of their four league phase games so far (L1). Building on that at the Etihad seems likely for a City side who’ve lost only two of their last 27 group/league phase UCL home matches (W22, D3), while a victory here would see them pick up three successive group/league phase wins in this competition for the first time since 2023/24. 

Napoli ended their three-match winless competitive run on Saturday night when they inched past Torino 1-0, although they remain firmly cut adrift in the early race for a top-four finish. Hitting the road is unlikely to provide much respite for a Napoli side who’ve won just one of their last seven competitive away matches (D1, L1), and their away-day struggles have been particularly evident in this competition. Having already lost at Villarreal and Porto in this season’s UCL, Napoli are now winless in eight away games in the competition (D1, L7), and another failure to win here will set a new unwanted club record.

Head-to-Head History

City have won each of the last three H2Hs after beating Napoli 2-0 in last season’s league phase. That was the most recent match in Napoli’s 11-match winless run away to English clubs (D3, L8).

Hot Stats and Streaks

City have scored nine of their 12 league phase goals this term in the second half. 

City have scored 2+ goals in 20 of their last 22 UCL home group stage/league phase games.

Napoli have been shown more cards than any other team in the UCL this season (YC12, RC1).

Only three sides have taken fewer corners than Napoli (11) in the league phase.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

A goal from City’s Rayan Cherki could lead to an entertaining affair given his last seven scoring appearances for club or country produced an average of over five goals per game! Kevin De Bruyne returns to face the club he made over 400 appearances for, with five of his last seven UCL goals - all scored for City - arriving after the hour mark. 

Matheus Nunes missed City’s game against Fulham, while Antonio Vergara added to a lengthy Napoli injury list when he was withdrawn early against Torino.

Betting Analysis

Neither side boasts particularly sparkling form, but Napoli’s recent record on the road in this competition (scoring in six of their eight-game winless streak) is enough to suggest there’s value in a City win and both teams to score.
