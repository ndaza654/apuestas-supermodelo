FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Two teams who were in form before the international break look to enhance their European qualification prospects as Eintracht Frankfurt host Hoffenheim.

Match News and Current Form

A late equaliser secured Frankfurt a 2-2 draw at Paderborn and extended their unbeaten run to three games (W2, D1) before the international break. That fixture did highlight the struggles the Eagles are having at the back though, with the 20 league goals they have conceded at this stage a joint-high for the club over the last 50 years. Only seven of those strikes have come at home, and a return to Waldstadion should be a boost to manager Adi Hütter given he has tasted defeat there just once in 23 games as Frankfurt boss (W14, D8), winning each of the last two. 

Hoffenheim’s miserable start to the campaign left them in 17th place after five matches, but they have surged up the Bundesliga table thanks to a run of three wins in four matches (L1). The most recent of those was a 2-1 success against Schalke, which actually ended a run of three without a victory (D1, L2) in all competitions as Hoffenheim continue to juggle European commitments. Manager Christian Ilzer therefore knows his side, who have four points fewer than at the same stage of last season, still have plenty of work to do, particularly having lost on five of their last seven away outings (W1, D1). 

Head-to-Head History

Hoffenheim had won just two of the last 17 H2Hs (D4, L11) before winning last season’s corresponding fixture 3-1. Seventeen of those 18 meetings saw both teams score.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Nine of Frankfurt’s 11 fixtures this term produced over 2.5 goals.

Frankfurt have dropped the Bundesliga’s joint-second most points from winning positions ahead of this round (nine).

A pre-round joint-league high eight Hoffenheim matches have featured over 8.5 corners.

Only one team enter this round having conceded more first-half goals than Hoffenheim’s 14.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Noah Atubolu has made 3+ saves in nine of his last ten games for Frankfurt, but a 3-0 defeat while playing for Freiburg last term means he has never kept a clean sheet in five personal H2Hs against Hoffenheim. Only one player enters this round having committed more Bundesliga fouls than Hoffenheim’s Ozan Kabak (19), including at least two in his last three league appearances.

Frankfurt will be missing the suspended Elias Baum, while Hoffenheim could be without Bambasé Conté and Robin Hranáč.
