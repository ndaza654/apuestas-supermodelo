FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

As Ligue 1 returns following the international break, second-placed Lille host Lyon, who could break into the top four as they begin life under new manager Carlos Vicens.

Match News and Current Form

Lille have lost just one league game this term (W6, D3), enjoying a terrific start to the campaign following Davide Ancelotti’s summer appointment. The Mastiffs were four games unbeaten across all competitions before the international break (W2, D2), and if they win this clash, it will mark their best start to a Ligue 1 season for 70 years. Having collected the joint second-most points in the division in 2026 alongside Lyon (50 each), Lille certainly look good value to secure that feat, particularly at home, where they’ve lost just one of their last 12 league matches (W5, D6).    

Lyon saw former boss Paulo Fonseca depart for Portuguese heavyweights Sporting prior to the international break, and in his absence, drew 1-1 with Real Sociedad before being beaten 2-0 at Brest. New boss Vicens inherits a side who’ve lost three of their last five competitive games (W1, D1), but a start to the season which saw them lose only three of their first 13 matches across all competitions certainly offers hope to the Spaniard (W7, D3). Vicens’ first task is to halt Les Gones’ winless away run (D1, L4), and if he does so, he would become the first Lyon manager to start their tenure with a win since Sylvinho in August 2019. 

Head-to-Head History

Lille have lost the last four H2Hs, suffering as many defeats as in the previous 23 meetings (W9, D10). The Mastiffs have never lost five straight H2Hs before.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Lille have recorded the second-most clean sheets in Ligue 1 this season pre-round (five).

Ten of Lille’s 14 games this term produced 3+ yellow cards.

None of Lyon’s last nine competitive matches were won by the away side on the day.

Twelve of Lyon’s 18 games this term produced over 9.5 total corners.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Lille youngster Soriba Diaoune has registered five goal involvements in as many games (G3, A2), with all three goals arriving before half-time. Lyon’s Ernest Nuamah has scored four goals in eight Ligue 1 appearances this term and could be another contender for an early strike having scored his last five for club or country in the first half.

Lille duo Hamza Igamane and Ayase Ueda are nearing returns. Lyon’s long-term absentees include Tyler Morton, Pavel Šulc and Loïs Openda.
