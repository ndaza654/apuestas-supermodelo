FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Parma need to arrest their slide down the Serie A table, but they face a daunting test against an in-form Roma side with their sights set on the title.

Match News and Current Form

An early red card inside ten minutes against Bologna set Parma on their way to a fourth defeat in six matches (W1, D1), leaving Alberto Gilardino’s side just a point above the relegation zone over the international break. Parma can take some comfort in also having eight points after 11 rounds last term before eventually surviving, and they’ll hope the similarities don’t stop there as matchday 12 yielded a victory last term! That did come on the road though, and their fortunes haven’t been quite as good at home of late, as one win from their last seven such outings evidences (D2, L4).

A three-match winning streak in Serie A in which they scored 12 goals has helped Roma close the gap at the top of the table, leaving them just two points behind city rivals Lazio pre-round. Fans can therefore continue dreaming of a first league title in 25 years, a belief further fuelled by the fact that Roma have never before scored as many as the 31 goals they rattled in across their opening 11 Serie A matches this term. The Giallorossi have also scored at least twice in their last three competitive away matches, but having also conceded 2+ goals in each of those (W1, D1, L1), goals aren’t necessarily conducive to success for Roma.

Head-to-Head History

Roma have won four consecutive H2Hs and six of their last eight visits to Parma (L2), although their last two away H2H wins did come by a single-goal margin.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Parma are the only Serie A side yet to score inside the opening 30 minutes.

Only Venezia have failed to score more often than Parma (five) in Serie A this season.

Roma have led a joint-league-high seven games at half-time this season (HT: D3, L1).

Ahead of the round, only Inter have scored more than Roma’s 16 second-half goals.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

After netting his first goal for Parma against Bologna, Vincent Sierro has now scored each of his last six club goals after the break. Rodrigo Mora has scored each of his last nine club goals in victories, with Roma netting at least three times in his four goalscoring appearances in Serie A.

Parma are without the suspended Abdoulaye Ndiaye as well as newly-sidelined duo David Romero and Diego Carlos. Roma are missing Lorenzo Pellegrini.

Betting Analysis

Parma’s distinct lack of goals coupled with Roma’s free-scoring nature makes a punt on Roma to win both halves seem appealing.
