FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

La Liga strugglers Valencia face a tough challenge to escape the relegation zone as they travel to a Rayo Vallecano side who have earned ten of their 12 points this season in home matches.

Match News and Current Form

The international break came at a good time for Rayo Vallecano, who have lost two of their last three La Liga games (W1), just one fewer than in their previous 15 such outings (W6, D6). The Red Sashes sit just two points clear of the relegation zone as a result, but things could be far different given that Beñat San José’s men have dropped a pre-round joint-league high ten points from winning positions. Just two of those were at home where, despite playing at two different venues, Rayo have been beaten only once in 14 La Liga matches (W8, D5), with six of those wins accompanied by a clean sheet.

The arrival of the league’s joint-lowest scorers points to that run extending, with Valencia’s struggles in front of goal contributing to them sitting inside the relegation zone. The arrival of Javier Aguirre hasn’t yet had the desired impact given that he’s overseen just one win in five La Liga matches in charge (D2, L2), but he may see this game as the perfect springboard as he’s unbeaten in seven personal meetings with Rayo (W5, D2). Los Che are also unbeaten in four competitive away games (W3, D1), and have been beaten in just two of their last eight league trips to face sides from the Community of Madrid (W2, D4). 

Head-to-Head History

Valencia have won just one of their last nine La Liga trips to Rayo (D5, L3), while ten of the last 16 top-flight meetings at any venue ended level (W3, L3).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Rayo are the only side yet to score beyond the 75th minute in La Liga this season.

At least two Rayo players were booked in each of their last seven games in all competitions.

Valencia have lost all six La Liga matches in which they conceded first this season.

Valencia have led just one league fixture at half-time this term (HT: D7, L4).

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Sergio Camello (G7) has scored more than double the number of La Liga goals any other Rayo player has managed this term, though just one of his five scoring appearances this season ended in victory (D2, L2). Luis Rioja could be in line for a start for Valencia after being involved in three goals (G1, A2) in their Copa del Rey win last time out, with four of his last six goals for the club coming in away games.

Óscar Valentín and Álvaro García missed Rayo’s last game, while Valencia’s José Gayà is suspended.
