FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Surprise Serie A leaders Lazio will hope that the international break has not stifled their momentum as they prepare to take on a Lecce side trending in the wrong direction.

Match News and Current Form

Lazio have won nine and drawn two of their 11 Serie A matches this season, meaning that they are one of just four unbeaten teams across Europe’s top five leagues in 2026/27 ahead of this round of fixtures. The Biancocelesti’s superb form has been built on a strong defence that has conceded a division-low nine goals this term, and they will hope to now return to winning ways after their 1-1 draw at Napoli before the break. The Stadio Olimpico faithful will welcome Gennaro Gattuso’s men back with open arms, as Lazio have treated them to five victories in their last six top-flight home games (D1).

Lecce will have taken the time away to work out what has been going wrong, having gone on a three-game losing streak followed by their own 1-1 draw - with Torino - on matchday 11. Eusebio Di Francesco’s men had not shared the spoils once in their previous 14 Serie A games (W6, L8), but another draw here would likely be most welcome, as they are sitting 14th, just three points above the relegation zone. Di Francesco will have to think hard about his team talk, not only because Lecce have won just one of their last five away matches (D1, L3), but also due to his record of no wins from his last ten managerial meetings with Lazio overall (D2, L8).

Head-to-Head History

Lazio have lost just one of the last five H2Hs (W3, D1) and have won 12 of the last 18 at home (D4, L2).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Lazio have opened the scoring in nine games this term (FT: W7, D2).

Five of Lazio’s last six home league outings saw both teams score.

The half-time result was replicated at full-time in 14 of Lecce’s last 15 games.

Lecce have conceded a league-high 13 first-half goals this season.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Lazio’s Tijjani Noslin has registered four goal contributions in his last three matches (G2, A2), but usually has an impact later on in games, with ten of his last 11 goals arriving after half-time, including one in last season’s corresponding fixture. Opposing #14 Youssef Maleh will try to help Lecce’s cause, as six of his last nine goals have come away from home.

Lazio will likely be without Josip Šutalo and Nuno Tavares, while Davide Frattesi and Adam Marušić are doubts. Lecce seemingly have no fresh absentees.

Betting Analysis

The hosts will look to capitalise on Lecce’s recent tendency to concede in the opening 45 minutes, making Lazio to be winning at half-time and full-time a wise choice here.
