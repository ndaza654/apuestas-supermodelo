FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Schalke are still looking for their first home goal of this Bundesliga campaign and look unlikely to get much respite from Stuttgart, who are eyeing up the European spots after their second clean sheet win of the Bundesliga season.

Match News and Current Form

Schalke’s 2-1 defeat to Hoffenheim before the international break extended their winless run to seven competitive matches (D2, L5) and kept them in the relegation zone with just six points, the same tally that the Royal Blues had after nine matches of their relegation-bound campaign in 2022/23. Head coach Miron Muslić is under pressure to move his side away from the danger zone and bring some long-awaited joy to the home supporters, who haven't seen their side score in four consecutive Bundesliga home outings. Extending that drought here would see Schalke make unwanted history by equalling St. Pauli’s all-time Bundesliga record of failing to score in the first five home games of a season.

Stuttgart earned their third consecutive win in domestic competitions when they defeated Werder Bremen 1-0 in their last match before the break. That included back-to-back wins in the Bundesliga, which earned the Reds as many wins and points as they had managed in the first seven matches of this season. Sebastian Hoeneß’ side have proven to be no pushovers on the road, winning four of their last six away matches in all competitions and establishing a 1-0 lead within half an hour in three of them, setting them up to draw first blood against their typically slow-starting opponents.

Head-to-Head History

This will be the 101st Bundesliga H2H and the first in more than three years. Schalke won the last meeting between these sides, forming the latest part of an impressive run of seven wins from the last 12 Bundesliga H2Hs (D4, L1).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Schalke failed to score before half-time in seven of their nine Bundesliga fixtures this season.

Only six of Schalke’s last 17 Bundesliga home games saw both teams score.

Six of Stuttgart’s last eight Bundesliga matches produced an odd number of goals.

Ten of Stuttgart’s last 12 away Bundesliga matches saw both teams score.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Schalke’s Junior Adamu could take his time to make an impact as ten of his last 14 goals for club and country were scored beyond the hour mark. Ermedin Demirović had three personal H2H contributions (G2, A1) in two matches when he last played against Schalke in the 2022/23 Bundesliga campaign. He is also likely to score late, as eight of his last 11 Bundesliga goals have also come beyond the 60th minute.

Several players for both teams could miss out here, including Schalke’s Timo Becker and Stuttgart pair Angelo Stiller and Josha Vagnoman.
