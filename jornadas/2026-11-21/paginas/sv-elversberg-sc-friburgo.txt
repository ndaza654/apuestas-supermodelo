FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Elversberg are enjoying the best start to a Bundesliga season points-wise of any newcomer after nine games since RB Leipzig (21) in 2016/17 and will look to continue building momentum against Freiburg, who are flying high in third.  

Match News and Current Form

Elversberg may have lost 2-1 to Borussia Dortmund just before the international break, but their tally of 13 Bundesliga points (W4, D1, L4) is still a great return for a team that many tipped to go straight back down. Indeed, since the 1970s, only one side has scored more goals at this stage in their first-ever season in the division (Hoffenheim in 2008/09 with 24), while only Bayern Munich and Leipzig have found the net more often on average in German top-flight home matches this term. Across this Bundesliga campaign, the Black and White have certainly shared the attacking load, with 14 players getting at least one goal involvement, a figure only eclipsed by Leipzig (15) ahead of this round of fixtures. 

Freiburg are somewhat unlikely early-season title challengers after accumulating their joint-highest points tally at this stage of a Bundesliga campaign (19), with the latest three coming against Union Berlin (2-0) on matchday nine. The Breisgau Brazilians have also scored more goals in the division (20) after nine games in a season than ever before, while they have found the net in their last 12 top-flight encounters - the longest ongoing run of any Bundesliga side. After collecting the majority of their points this season at home (12 vs 7), they’ll now be desperate to stay in the title race by registering back-to-back league wins on the road for the first time since mid-April. 

Head-to-Head History

Freiburg’s 2-0 victory in the 2009/10 DFB-Pokal is the only previous H2H.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Noel Futkeu got his first Elversberg goal against Dortmund - the sixth time in his last 12 scoring appearances that he has been on the losing side (W5, D1). Freiburg's Yuito Suzuki got his fifth goal of the season against Union, and among all the players to have registered at least ten attempts in the Bundesliga this term, he has the second-best pre-round shot conversion rate (38.5%). 

Elversberg defender Lukas Pinckert went off injured against Dortmund. Freiburg goalkeeper Mio Backhaus has been nursing a thumb problem and youngster Berkay Yılmaz has lacked match fitness.
