FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Coventry and Crystal Palace have both started their Premier League campaigns poorly, giving this the feel of a ‘relegation six-pointer’, which the visitors approach under new management.

Match News and Current Form

Coventry ended the last matchday just one place above the relegation zone, having lost at least seven of their opening ten league games of a campaign for only the third time in their history. Winning three of their last six league fixtures (L3) offers some hope but it also highlights their unpredictable nature, something that’s been particularly clear at home, where Frank Lampard’s men have won two league games by 2+ goals and lost the other three without scoring. A good start appears key for the Sky Blues then, as they’re one of two sides yet to recover a point from a losing position in the Premier League this season.

Bottom-of-the-table Crystal Palace took action during the international break after a dreadful start to the campaign, appointing former Brentford and Tottenham boss Thomas Frank to replace Pierre Sage. The Dane inherits a side who have suffered four consecutive league defeats and conceded 24 goals already, their highest tally at this stage since 1980/81. He may also have his work cut out to make an instant impact given that the Eagles have won just one away league fixture this term (D1, L3), drawing blanks in four of those games, while only two of Palace’s last 13 permanent managers won the first Premier League game they managed.

Head-to-Head History

This will be the first H2H since March 2012, with Coventry losing just one of the last four meetings (W2, D1), and unbeaten across the last four meetings they hosted (W2, D2).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Coventry received under 1.5 yellow cards in seven of their ten Premier League fixtures this season.

Coventry haven't conceded in the opening 30 minutes of a home league game this term.

Thomas Frank has never won his first game in charge of a senior club (D1, L2).

Palace possess the Premier League’s worst away first-half record this season (HT: D1, L4).

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Jack Rudoni has scored in three of Coventry’s last five league fixtures, leading his side for Premier League shots taken and chances created this term (both 17). After being booked in three of his last five league appearances, Palace’s Jefferson Lerma has collected more yellow cards (57) than all but two other South Americans in the Premier League - Pablo Zabaleta (68) and Joelinton (58).

Coventry appear to have no new injury concerns while Palace are without Jean-Philippe Mateta through suspension and Jørgen Strand Larsen is doubtful after suffering an injury while on duty with Norway.

Betting Analysis

With Coventry failing to score in three home league games this term, and Palace doing likewise four times on the road, backing against both teams to score could be worth pursuing.
