FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Newcastle United go in search of a much-needed Premier League victory against table-toppers Arsenal, who will be looking to extend their excellent start to this season’s title defence.

Match News and Current Form

Newcastle United went into the latest international break struggling for winning consistency and on a run of just two victories across their last eight Premier League fixtures (D2, L4) following a 1-1 draw against Fulham. That form saw them enter this matchday a fraction off the European qualification pace in 12th spot, and a return to St James’ Park may not be welcome news as no side has lost more home top-flight matches than the Magpies in 2026 (seven). Their poor run even includes two successive 1-0 defeats on home soil, as many goalless displays as they had recorded across their prior 35 league home fixtures combined.

With nine wins from their opening ten league outings this season (L1), Arsenal have made a near-perfect start to their Premier League title defence. Another win would see them become just the fifth team to reach ten victories in as few as 11 games to start a campaign, and with each of those prior four teams going on to lift the title in the same season, the omens would be looking very good for the Gunners if they achieve that feat. Manager Mikel Arteta could also record a bit of personal history here too in the away dugout, as he has the opportunity to record a 159th Premier League victory and enter the top ten for most in the history of the competition. 

Head-to-Head History

Arsenal have won more Premier League games against only two sides than they have against Newcastle (38), though have lost three of their last five away league H2Hs.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Newcastle’s last four Premier League matches all produced under 2.5 goals.

Newcastle have won 4+ corners in eight of their ten league games this term.

Just two of Arsenal’s last nine away league matches saw both teams score.

Arsenal have faced the fewest shots on target in the Premier League this season (26).

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Five of Harvey Barnes’ six goal contributions this season for Newcastle have come at home, while 11 of his last 13 strikes overall have fallen on home soil. Arsenal’s Martin Ødegaard has three goal contributions across his last five league H2Hs (G1, A2), with each of his five strikes this season coming after half-time.

Joelinton remains unavailable for Newcastle, while Arsenal are missing the suspended Max Dowman and injured Bukayo Saka.
