FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Only PSG have collected more points than Nice across the last six Ligue 1 rounds, so the meeting of two in-form sides has the potential to be the game of the round. 

Match News and Current Form

Nice can profess to be one of Ligue 1’s most in-form sides at present after their 2-0 win against Angers prior to the international break secured a fifth victory in six top-flight matches (L1). That marks a remarkable turnaround in form for a side who’d only won five of their previous 34 Ligue 1 games (D13, L16) and affords them a shot at winning four matches in a row in this competition for the first time since March 2025. Three home league wins in succession should give them some confidence here, but they’ll have to overcome the psychological hurdle of failing to win any of their last seven Saturday-held league matches (D4, L3) if they’re to stretch that run to four for the first time since December 2023.

PSG may have made a slow start to the Ligue 1 campaign but it didn’t take them long to hit their stride and they now sit pretty in their customary position at the top of the table. Seven league wins in succession explain why, with those part of a wider run of 11 in a row in all competitions, the longest run across the top-five European leagues pre-round as the Parisians’ quest for a sixth Ligue 1 crown in succession gathers momentum. An impressive 14 wins from their last 16 Saturday-held away league games (L2) will certainly fuel belief they can keep that momentum going here, especially given one of them was a 4-0 triumph in last season's corresponding fixture. 

Head-to-Head History

PSG’s win in this fixture last term is one of seven they’ve managed in their last nine visits to Nice (D1, L1), while their pair of wins to nil in last season’s H2Hs allows them the chance to keep three successive Ligue 1 clean sheets against Nice for the first time ever.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Only PSG (66) have committed fewer fouls than Nice (95) in Ligue 1 this season.

Nice have collected a pre-round league-high 24 yellow cards this term.

PSG have scored in their last 23 competitive away games. 

PSG have netted 12 of their 15 Ligue 1 away goals this season in the second half. 

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Nice's Jonathan Clauss is set to make his 200th Ligue 1 appearance here - three of his last four goals for the club arrived after the hour mark, while he’s attempted the second most league shots of any Nice player this term. Ferran Torres has racked up more shots on target (15) than any other PSG player in Ligue 1 this season and has netted six of his seven goals for the club after the half-hour mark. 

Mohamed Abdelmonem is expected to miss out here for Nice. PSG’s Désiré Doué picked up an injury for France on international duty, but Nuno Mendes, Marquinhos and Ousmane Dembélé are all closing in on returns.
