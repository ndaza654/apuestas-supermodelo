FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Monaco will be looking to regain their spot in Ligue 1’s top six after a run of poor results as they face Auxerre, who are in danger of getting sucked into the relegation zone.

Match News and Current Form

After starting their Ligue 1 campaign with four wins from five matches (D1), Monaco have since gone five such games without a victory (D3, L2) and last went longer without one in September 2019. Filipe Luís’ men therefore find themselves seventh and out of the European qualification places, somewhat ironic given that they have won all five of their UEFA Conference League matches so far this season. A return to Stade Louis II after two winless away league games (D1, L1) may be just what the Monégasques need to get their campaign back on track though, as they haven't lost any of their last seven home matches across all competitions (W5, D2), and have scored 2+ goals in 13 of their last 15 such games.

Auxerre needed the international break after losing back-to-back Ligue 1 matches, falling to 5-1 and 1-0 defeats against Strasbourg and Paris FC respectively. AJA therefore begin the weekend in 14th place, just two points above the relegation zone, although given that they have the league’s worst defence this season with 24 goals conceded before this round of fixtures, it is perhaps surprising that they aren't further down the table. Auxerre are often a threat going forward though, and could score in a club-record tenth successive top-flight away match if they find the net here (W2, D2, L5). 

Head-to-Head History

Monaco are unbeaten in the last 12 H2Hs (W9, D3), scoring 2+ goals in ten of those games.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Just one of Monaco’s last eight league matches was decided by a 2+ goal margin.

Seven of Monaco’s last eight top-flight games saw both teams score.

Auxerre have conceded first eight times this season, a pre-round league-high figure.

Auxerre have conceded a pre-round league-high 17 second-half goals this season.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Monaco may look to give Folarin Balogun a start here as he scored in last season’s corresponding fixture, but he may also prove to be a dangerous substitute, with five of his last six goals at club level arriving after half-time. Auxerre may turn to Danny Namaso to inspire them, as four of his last five Ligue 1 goals have come away from home.

Monaco’s Aleksandr Golovin went off injured on matchday ten against Le Mans (1-1), while Auxerre are set to welcome back Lamfia Dioubaté from suspension.
