FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Mid-table battlers Alavés and newly-promoted Deportivo A Coruña will meet for the first time in six years as both sides look to stay in touch with La Liga’s top six.

Match News and Current Form

Alavés stormed out of the blocks this season with impressive wins in each of their first three La Liga home games, but they’ve since badly struggled, failing to win any of their last eight games in this competition (D3, L5). That’s their worst winless streak in the top flight since a run of 11 in 2021/22, and means Quique Sánchez Flores’ men spent the international break just three points clear of the relegation zone. There’s no guarantee that a return home will offer any respite given that Alavés failed to beat relegation contenders Valencia (L 1-0) and Málaga (D 1-1) during that winless period.

Deportivo A Coruña have made a mockery of their status as pre-season relegation favourites by losing just three of their first 12 league games (W3, D6), helping them sit inside the top half and within striking distance of the European places. Things could have been far better for the Herculeans were it not for a run of one win from their last eight league games (D4, L3), although all three defeats therein came against sides starting the round inside the top six. Two of those came on the road, where Deportivo are winless in four in the league (D2, L2), and winning just one of their last 14 La Liga games away to Basque clubs (D6, L7) hints at further travelling pain here. 

Head-to-Head History

Alavés have won seven of the last ten La Liga H2Hs they hosted (D1, L2), keeping a clean sheet in each of the last three. This will be the first top-flight meeting between the sides since February 2018.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Only two of Alavés’ last 11 league games were decided by a 2+ goal margin.

Alavés trailed at half-time in only one of their five La Liga losses this season.

Ten of Deportivo’s 15 league goals this season came after half-time.

Eight of Deportivo’s 12 La Liga matches this season produced under 2.5 goals.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Mariano Díaz is Alavés’ most recent goalscorer in La Liga with the third of his four strikes in the competition this season to come beyond the 80th minute. After he equalised in the win over Osasuna on matchday 11, Mario Soriano’s last four goals for Deportivo were all the team’s first on the day and each came after half-time. 

Neither side look to have any fresh injury concerns.
