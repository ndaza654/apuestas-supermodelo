FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

After one of their worst-ever starts to a Bundesliga season, Union Berlin will be hoping for better fortunes after the international break when they host an in-form RB Leipzig side. 

Match News and Current Form

After collecting just seven points from their opening nine games (W1, D4), Union Berlin have made their joint second-worst start to a Bundesliga campaign. Their defensive record makes for worse reading though, as the 21 goals they’ve conceded at this point are a club-record total at this stage of a top-flight campaign. It’s unsurprising then that Mauro Lustrinelli’s team are without a victory from their last five competitive games (D3, L2), but their home form offers a glimmer of hope as Union Berlin have lost just one of their last seven Bundesliga outings at Stadion An der Alten Försterei (W2, D4), a venue where they have collected 86% of their points this season.

A five-match losing run on the road from early September to mid-October now feels like little more than a distant memory for an RB Leipzig side who arrive in Berlin targeting a third successive away win across all competitions. Martín Demichelis’ side have won three league games in a row for the first time this season, the only team in the division to take nine points across those matchdays, points which have thrust them into the early title running. A free-scoring attack has helped them on their way, with only three other managers of any team in Bundesliga history seeing their side score more in their first nine games in this competition.

Head-to-Head History

Union Berlin have won only one of the last six meetings between the sides (D2, L3), but when hosting Leipzig they’ve had the upper hand (W5, D2, L2).

Hot Stats and Streaks

None of Union’s last five Bundesliga games produced over 2.5 goals.

Union Berlin have picked up at least one first-half yellow card in each of their last eight home games.

Leipzig trailed at half-time in three of their four away league games this season (HT: W1).

Leipzig haven’t dropped a point when scoring first in the Bundesliga this term (W5).

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Tim Skarke scored in stoppage time in this fixture last season and both of his goals in this campaign also arrived after the 90th minute, contributing to eight of his last nine club strikes coming in the second half. Rômulo got RB Leipzig’s second in the last H2H and has seen all five of his goals this term arrive on or beyond the hour mark.

Union hope to have Aljoscha Kemlein available again, but Leipzig will be without Marc Guiu.
