FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Ipswich Town visit one of the three teams they defeated on the road during their previous Premier League campaign, and another victory here would see them move ahead of Tottenham in the table. 

Match News and Current Form

Tottenham’s return to the top half lasted just one round as their four-game unbeaten run ended with a 1-0 loss to Leeds, who jumped above them in the table. Manager Roberto de Zerbi gave a sharp and public rebuke of his players after the match and now needs to find a way to quickly reinvigorate a team that haven't won any of their last four Premier League matches immediately following an international break (D1, L3). There have been recent signs of incremental improvement at home in this competition, illustrated by wins over Coventry (3-1) and Crystal Palace (2-0), so upcoming home games against Ipswich and Fulham could send them into December’s North London derby in strong home form.

Ipswich went into an international break on the back of a defeat for the second time this season, going down 2-1 at home to Bournemouth in their last outing to record a fourth straight Premier League game without victory (D2, L2). They spent the break with a three-point buffer from the relegation zone only due to having collected all 11 of their points against clubs to also have 11 or fewer points at the start of this round. Their 2-2 draw at Hull in their last Premier League away trip was the highest-placed opposition they haven’t lost to this season and defensive issues are largely to blame for their troubles, as this game could see them become the fifth team in Premier League history to concede in 30 consecutive matches. 

Head-to-Head History

These teams traded away victories in 2024/25, with Tottenham ending a four-game losing streak to Ipswich in the process.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Sixteen of the 21 goals in Tottenham's league matches this term arrived in the second half.

Tottenham have scored the opening goal only three times this Premier League season (W3).

Ipswich are yet to win a second half on the road this Premier League season (2H: D3, L2).

A league-high 80% of Ipswich’s Premier League games saw both teams score. 

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Tottenham’s Omar Marmoush and Ipswich’s Julio Enciso both scored for their countries during the international break. Marmoush is looking to extend a streak that saw six of his last eight club goals come in home wins, while Enciso’s last ten goals for club or country were scored beyond the half-hour mark.

Tottenham will assess Sávio, Mathys Tel, Mykhailo Mudryk and others closer to matchday, while Ipswich appear to have a clean bill of health.
