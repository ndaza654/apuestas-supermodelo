FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

As the Premier League returns following the international break, Chelsea are in search of a much-needed victory as they host Leeds, whose wait for a win at Stamford Bridge stretches back 27 years.

Match News and Current Form

Chelsea exited the EFL Cup and slipped out of the Premier League’s top four as they lost three of their last four matches across all competitions before the international break (W1), but they have made a positive start to the season on the whole. Following the summer appointment of Xabi Alonso, there appears little reason why the Blues’ objective of a return to Europe cannot be achieved this term, with only one home league defeat suffered in that time (W3, D1). Chelsea have consequently enjoyed their best start to a top-flight campaign at Stamford Bridge since 2022/23, and their best under a new manager since Maurizio Sarri in 2018/19.

Leeds had been winless in five before a morale-boosting 1-0 home victory over Tottenham before the international break (D3, L2). The ninth-placed Whites have still only been beaten twice in the Premier League this term (W3, D5), and seem a far cry from a side that could become victims of 'second-season syndrome', instead looking upwards as they sit just two points behind Chelsea. Perhaps unsurprisingly, both their league losses this campaign have come away from Elland Road (W1, D2), and while this would be an ideal time to improve their record on their travels, boss Daniel Farke is winless in his three previous managerial meetings overall with Alonso (D1, L2).

Head-to-Head History

Leeds haven't won at Chelsea since December 1999, losing eight of their ten competitive away H2Hs since then, including September’s EFL Cup tie (6-3).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Only one of Chelsea’s last six league matches saw both teams score.

Ahead of this round, Chelsea have conceded the second-most set-piece goals across Europe’s top-five leagues this season (eight).

Leeds have conceded the third-fewest goals in the Premier League this term (eight).

Leeds were shown exactly two yellow cards in five of their last six matches.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Chelsea midfielder Moisés Caicedo is yet to lose to Leeds (W3, D3), but he has picked up five yellow cards in those six H2Hs! Leeds’ Ethan Ampadu makes for a tenacious midfield opponent, having been booked in five of his last six games for club or country.

Chelsea have an extensive list of absentees, but first-choice goalkeeper Emiliano Martínez should be back available and João Pedro could also return. Leeds will hope to see Anton Stach return from injury.
