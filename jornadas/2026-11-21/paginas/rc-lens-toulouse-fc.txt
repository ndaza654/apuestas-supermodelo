FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Didier Digard takes the reins at Lens and will hope for a good start given the club’s impressive recent record against Toulouse, who sit level on points with their hosts.

Match News and Current Form

Form prior to the international break made for dismal reading for Lens, who lost three competitive games in a row, including the heaviest defeat in club history when they were thumped 9-1 by Como. The Blood and Gold made their second change of manager already this season as a result, with Yannick Cahuzac replaced by Digard, who only recently departed Le Havre. He takes charge of a side which has already lost eight games in this current campaign, as many as they suffered in the entirety of last season, and further ignominy could await here as another reversal would see them lose three competitive home games in succession for the first time since April 2011 - a streak that included a defeat to Toulouse!

The international break arguably came at a bad time for Toulouse, who had momentum on their side, losing just one of their last seven Ligue 1 games (W3, D3). A 3-1 win over Strasbourg in their last outing affords the Violets the chance to post back-to-back league victories for the first time this season, and hitting the road seems to aid the case of a side who’ve lost just one of their last seven top-flight road trips (W2, D4). That defeat did come in their last such outing though and also formed part of a dismal record this season against sides who join Lens in starting this round above them in the table (D3, L3).

Head-to-Head History

Lens have won eight of the last ten Ligue 1 H2Hs (D1, L1) and five of the last six competitive meetings they hosted.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Ahead of the round, only Marseille have scored more second-half home league goals than Lens (7) this season.

Six of Lens’ last seven league matches produced over 9.5 corners.

Toulouse committed the second-most fouls in Ligue 1 this season pre-round (158).

Toulouse have scored 13 of their 15 league goals this term after half-time.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

The referee may have to keep a close eye on Lens’ Ruben Aguilar, who has committed a team-high 14 fouls in Ligue 1 this season. Cristian Cásseres takes that crown for Toulouse (18), while his six assists this term are already his most in a single league campaign!

Lens’ Andrija Bulatović picked up an injury on international duty, while Thomas Jørgensen pulled out of the Denmark U21 squad through injury and could miss out here.

Betting Analysis

Lens have had two weeks to stew on their nosedive in form, and with an impressive H2H record in tow, a home win seems a logical pick.
