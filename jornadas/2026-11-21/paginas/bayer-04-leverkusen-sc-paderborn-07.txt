FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Free-scoring Bayer Leverkusen look to tighten up their defence against a Paderborn side who have proved surprisingly resilient since returning to the Bundesliga.

Match News and Current Form

Leverkusen’s 2-1 win at Cologne prior to the international break was a timely return to winning ways following back-to-back Bundesliga defeats, and potentially a step towards more consistency and better defensive solidity. Conceding 17 times already, their highest tally at this stage since 2018/19, has undermined Die Werkself’s attacking threat, yet they remain in a European qualifying position. Leverkusen have had fewer issues at the BayArena than on the road as Carles Martínez Novell’s side have won three of their four Bundesliga home fixtures this term (L1), scoring at least twice on each occasion, although they have lost each of their last two matches here in all competitions.

Paderborn have made an encouraging start since returning to the Bundesliga via the play-offs and are now unbeaten in a joint-club record four top-flight matches (W1, D3) after drawing 2-2 with Frankfurt last time out. Keeping two clean sheets across their last three league games highlights growing defensive strength for Ralf Kettemann’s side, as does conceding just five goals on the road in the league this term, more than only Dortmund (four). Turning those solid defensive performances into victories remains a challenge, as they’re still winless in Bundesliga away games this campaign (D3, L1).

Head-to-Head History

Leverkusen are undefeated across all seven previous H2Hs (W6, D1), winning the last six. Both previous top-flight meetings here produced over 3.5 goals.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Leverkusen’s Bundesliga fixtures have produced an average of 4.11 goals per game this term.

Leverkusen are unbeaten across their last ten home league games against promoted sides (W7, D3).

Paderborn’s last four Bundesliga goals, and five of their eight this term, came from set-pieces.

Paderborn’s 11 competitive games this season produced over 7.5 corners.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Aleix García’s match opener against Cologne was his third successive Leverkusen goal to arrive before the tenth minute and took his goal involvement tally to four in their last four games (G1, A3), while he was also booked in two of their last three games. Two of Stefano Marino’s three goals for Paderborn this term came away from home and four of his last five arrived before half-time.

Leverkusen may be sweating on the availability of Ibrahim Maza, who returned home from international duty with Algeria early with an ankle injury. Paderborn appear to have no fresh injury concerns.

Betting Analysis

Leverkusen’s attacking threat at home and their league games this season averaging more than four goals per game suggests backing over 3.5 goals is a viable option.
