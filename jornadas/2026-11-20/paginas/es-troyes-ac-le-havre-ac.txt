FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Stade de l’Aube hosts a Friday-night Ligue 1 ‘basement battle’ as 17th-placed Troyes host 18th-placed Le Havre, who arrive with a new head coach in charge.

Match News and Current Form

Last year’s Ligue 2 champions Troyes have struggled with the step up to the top flight, winning just two of their league games this season (D2, L6), although four points and two clean sheets from their last three fixtures could be seen as an encouraging sign of improvement (W1, D1, L1). While winning just one of their five home league games this term isn’t much to be excited by (D1, L3), that sole victory came in their last outing here when they defeated Le Mans 2-0, so there is hope that better times are ahead after a slow start that saw them ahead of only Schalke (five) for goals scored in Europe’s top-five leagues this season (seven).

Le Havre entered the break six points adrift at the bottom after making the joint-second worst ten-game start in Ligue 1 history, and the club responded by sacking Didier Digard and appointing Valérien Ismaël during the international window. It could be a tough French managerial debut for Ismaël given Le Havre remain winless in this competition (D2, L8) despite the effort that has produced more shot attempts in Ligue 1 (166) than any team except PSG (193). With 22 goals conceded, fewer only than Auxerre (24), Ismaël needs to work on both Le Havre’s defending and their resilience on the road, where they won just one of their last 16 away league fixtures (D6, L9).

Head-to-Head History

Le Havre have lost just two of the last seven H2Hs (W4, D1), although Troyes have won three of the last four meetings they hosted (L1).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Troyes failed to score in a league-high six Ligue 1 fixtures this season.

Troyes have conceded exactly one goal in each of their last four home league games played on a Friday (W2, D1, L1).

Le Havre have lost only two of their last 12 Ligue 1 games against newly promoted clubs (W4, D6).

Only PSG (six) have scored more goals after the 75th minute in away Ligue 1 matches this season than Le Havre (four).

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Antoine Mille will be watched closely after being booked beyond the 80th minute in three of his last six appearances for Troyes. Lamine N'Diaye scored in Le Havre’s last away game (L 4-3 at Marseille) and has been booked after half-time in two of his last four appearances. 

Junior Diaz is suspended for Troyes due to yellow card accumulation while Le Havre appear to have no new major injury concerns.
