FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Athletic Club will be pursuing a provisional move into La Liga’s top six when they host an Espanyol side sitting dangerously close to the relegation zone.

Match News and Current Form

Athletic Bilbao return from the international break looking to extend a four-match unbeaten run (W3, D1) that includes a 1-1 draw at Osasuna earlier this month. That run of form means they sit seventh in the table, with a haul of 16 points that is lower than it was at this stage of four of their last five campaigns. A run of five games without defeat at home (W3, D2), during which they kept three clean sheets, should give Edin Terzić’s men reason to believe they can extend their undefeated streak here, particularly as the Lions tend to kick off the weekend strongly with eight wins from their last nine Friday-held La Liga fixtures (D1).

Eder Sarabia’s promising start to his new career as Espanyol boss continued when he followed up a 1-0 win at Villarreal on his managerial debut with a 0-0 draw against Deportivo A Coruña a fortnight ago. Those two results leave the club chasing not only three successive La Liga clean sheets for the first time in 2026, but also the chance to end an 11-month wait for back-to-back away victories. Their goalscoring in this part of the country suggests they’re in with a chance of achieving the latter as the Parakeets have scored in ten consecutive league matches on the road against Basque teams, but the fruits of their labour may take some time to arrive as Espanyol’s last four league fixtures have been 0-0 at the break.

Head-to-Head History

Athletic Bilbao have beaten Espanyol more times (88) than any other opponent (D42, L71), but lost both H2Hs last season.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Athletic conceded only one first-half goal across their last five home league outings.

Athletic are winless in La Liga this season when conceding first (D1, L3).

Espanyol have received the most yellow cards of any team this La Liga season (39).

Espanyol are winless in the six league games in which they have conceded first this season (D1, L5).

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Álex Berenguer has scored in three of his last four appearances for Athletic, while ten of his last 11 strikes have been the first or last of the game. Ángel Fortuño has been a revelation since taking the gloves for Espanyol, keeping two clean sheets in five games and registering the highest save percentage in La Liga this term (86%).

Athletic Bilbao’s Nico Serrano is doubtful, while Maroan Sannadi is out. Espanyol have no major new injury concerns.
