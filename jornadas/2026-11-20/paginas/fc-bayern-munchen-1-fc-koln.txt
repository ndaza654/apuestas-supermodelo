FLASHSCORE PREVIEW

Bayern Munich will look to establish at least a temporary buffer at the top of the Bundesliga standings against a severely out-of-sorts Cologne, who could be at risk of dropping into the bottom-three with another defeat.

Match News and Current Form

Bayern entered the international window at the top of the Bundesliga pile by virtue of goal difference alone, and with title rivals Dortmund heading to last-placed Mönchengladbach, they’ll likely need a victory here just to remain level on points. Bayern will be buoyed by the return of manager Vincent Kompany to work this week after a bout of meningitis that left him hospitalised, having performed admirably in his absence as the only side across Europe’s big five leagues to remain unbeaten in all competitions (W11, D5), which includes an incredible average of five goals per Bundesliga game at home.

The international break arguably came at the right time for Cologne after they failed to win across the previous four rounds of competition, scoring only twice throughout those four matches (D2, L2). They could end the weekend in the bottom three if they lose here, and playing away is unlikely to assist their cause as the Billy Goats arrive here on the back of having unwillingly set a joint-club record of 17 winless Bundesliga trips (D7, L10). Extending that miserable drought appears likely here on the basis of Cologne having earned the fewest points of any side playing in each of the Bundesliga’s last 34 rounds (24), including a 5-1 loss in their last visit here.

Head-to-Head History

Bayern are currently enjoying their second-longest active unbeaten H2H streak in the Bundesliga against Cologne (W20, D2), only possessing a longer ongoing sequence against Schalke (24 games). The hosts have accumulated 20 goals across their last six home games against Cologne (W5, D1).

Hot Stats and Streaks

Bayern have conceded just one first-half goal this Bundesliga season.

Bayern scored the opening goal in a Bundesliga-high seven of the first nine rounds (W6, D1). 

Eight of Cologne’s ten league goals this season arrived after half-time. 

Cologne led at half-time in only one of their nine league games this term (HT: D3, L5). 

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Lennart Karl has scored in each of Bayern’s last two competitive matches after doing so in just one of his 16 prior club appearances. He has never scored in three straight games for the club. Malek El Mala has contributed to Cologne’s last two goals despite playing a combined 33 minutes across those two games (G1, A1), and his most recent assist was for his brother and Cologne’s top league scorer, Said El Mala. 

Alphonso Davies suffered a hamstring injury with Canada and will be absent for Bayern here, while Cologne seemingly have no fresh concerns. 

Betting Analysis

Bayern are rightful favourites here and should realistically put Cologne to the sword, so backing Bayern to win by 3+ goals could squeeze some value out of their favouritism.
